# 0.21.0

## Abuse prevention

Free moves stay once per shop, now even after the account that claimed them is deleted. Migration 0105's `storefront_grant_record` survives erasure. New sign-ups are screened: throwaway email domains are refused, the sixth account from one address in a day is refused, and a suspended person's email is refused. The new linkage ledger (`account_link_signal`, 0105) keeps keyed digests of shops, desktop installs, sign-in addresses, browsers, email domains and cards. A nightly and on-event scorer raises flags (`abuse_flag`, 0106) for accounts that share a shop, device or card, for sign-up bursts, for throwaway domains, and for an unlink within seven days of spending free moves (remaining free moves are zeroed). Operators review flags at `/admin/abuse` and choose Dismiss, Warn (mail), Limit (no free moves, no new connections) or Ban. A ban ends sessions, answers every route with 403 "This account is suspended. Email contact@teachouse.io.", and refuses the account's email, shops, devices and cards for 24 months. Address and browser digests are kept 90 days. Design note: `docs/notes/design/abuse-prevention.md`.

Deploy:
- Run migrations 0105 and 0106.
- tam-auth needs no new secret: it derives `TAM_AUTH_ABUSE_SCREEN_URL` from `TAM_AUTH_CONSENT_URL`. Set it explicitly only if the consent URL is not `…/internal/consent`.
- tam-server must run with `--blob-kek-path`, otherwise no signals are digested.
- The nightly pass starts one minute after boot.
