# Teachouse 0.21.0

## Legal

- **The goodbye goes last.** `DELETE /v1/account` now captures the address and name when tam-auth confirms the proof, cancels the plan, deletes the identity, erases the organisation and commits the `account_deletion` row, and only then sends the goodbye. A deletion that stops halfway sends nothing. If the relay refuses the mail, the deletion stands, the failure is logged, and the answer says `goodbye_sent: false`. The mail is rewritten in the founder's voice: subject "Goodbye from Teachouse", greeting by first name, thanks for teaching with us, everything tied to the account deleted (account and sign-in, catalogue, shop connections, devices; plan cancelled), what stays (the terms they agreed to, a note of the deletion, billing records for tax), TPT and Tes listings and device files untouched, welcome back any time with a "Sign up again" button, a good-luck line, and "reply if this wasn't you".
- **Automated access (terms section 8).** New section: no scraping, crawling or bulk downloading of the site, the console, the API or other sellers' listings. No use of Teachouse content to train or feed AI. No bots, scripts, headless browsers or AI agents against the console or the API; the Teachouse app and an ordinary browser are the ways in. API use only through keys we issue. No giving a sign-in, session or key to a tool, service or outsider. We may rate-limit, challenge, block, suspend or close.
- **One person, one free account (terms section 5).** Extra accounts made for free moves or previews, sharing or moving a shop to get its free moves again, and evading a limit or suspension count as abuse. The possible actions are a warning, losing free moves, a stop on new connections, suspension or a ban. A ban can cover the email, shops, devices and cards, on any account. Moves and packs are frozen while suspended and forfeited on closure for abuse. A person reviews every flag. The wording matches the abuse-prevention slice's behaviour.
- **Renumbering.** The two new sections move the old sections 5–21 down to 6–23. Refunds is now section 11. Every in-page cross-reference has been updated, and links use anchors (`/terms/#refunds`, `#automated`, `#one-account`). Refunds gains the sentence "Months are counted in New Zealand time from the day your yearly plan started." and an "Ask for a refund in Account → Billing" route, as the refunds slice asked. Section 16 now points to self-service deletion instead of "email us".
- **Privacy.** A new "Abuse-detection records" subsection (`#abuse`) lists what is kept, all of it as keyed one-way hashes: IP and browser at sign-up and sign-in, app device ID, shop identity, Stripe card fingerprint, and the email domain (kept in plain). Request metadata (time, path, IP, user agent) is logged to detect automation. Retention: IP and browser hashes 90 days. Other signals last as long as the account. A note that a shop has had its free moves is kept while the free plan exists. A suspended account's email, shop, device and card hashes are kept for 24 months. The legitimate-interests basis now covers abuse detection, and the summary table and the Cloudflare row are updated.
- **Bots.** `tam_api::crawlers::AI_CRAWLERS` is the ai.robots.txt list (181 agents, commit `9ad8a47e23f7`, 3 October 2026). It feeds:
  - the landing `robots.txt`, through `just web-typegen` → `legal.generated.js` `REFUSED_CRAWLERS`. Everything is disallowed for those agents except `Applebot` (Apple search) and `facebookexternalhit` (Facebook link previews); `*` keeps `Allow: /` and the existing disallows.
  - the console host's `robots.txt`, which names every agent and then `*`, all `Disallow: /`.
  - a tam-server middleware (`refuse_crawlers`, just inside the host split) that answers 403 to any of those user agents on `/v1/*` and console paths on any host, and on every path of the console host. `robots.txt` and the landing's pages, assets and installers stay readable. Nine names too broad to substring-match (`Code`, `Cursor`, `Spider` and others) are left to `robots.txt`. Matching is one case-insensitive Aho–Corasick pass.

  Every answer now carries `X-Robots-Tag: noai, noimageai`. Landing pages add `<meta name="robots" content="noai, noimageai">`, and the console shell and maintenance page meta become `noindex, noai, noimageai`. `served-artefacts` in `flake.nix` checks that the landing `robots.txt` names GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot and Google-Extended.
- `TERMS_VERSION` and `PRIVACY_VERSION` move to `2026-10-05`, so every signed-in person accepts the terms again.

Deploy: no migrations. Run `just web-typegen` if the landing `legal.generated.js` is stale. Founder steps:

2. Terms section 20 promises 30 days' email notice before a change that matters. The new sections restrict use, so send that notice, or decide the abuse and automated-access rules are clarifications of what section 7 already said.
3. Refresh `AI_CRAWLERS` from ai.robots.txt now and then. Copy the list and move the commit noted in `crates/tam-api/src/crawlers.rs`.
