# Teachouse 0.21.0

## Refunds

The refund policy in the terms is now worked out by the code, not by an operator with a calculator. The Payments page's refund panel opens with the policy's amount and the sentence that explains it, and sellers can ask for a refund from Billing.

- **The formula.** Move Pack: refunded in full if none of its moves has been used and it was bought less than 14 days ago (the 14 days end at the same time of day 14 New Zealand days later). Monthly plan: a month that has started is not refunded. Yearly plan: `refund = yearly price × max(0, unused whole months − 1) ÷ 12`, rounded down to the cent. Months are New Zealand calendar months counted from the day the plan's year started, and a month is used from the instant it starts. The price is what the charge took. `crates/tam-api/src/refund_policy.rs`, `policy_refund`, with a unit test per edge (day 1, month 11, month 12, the instant a month starts, 31 January and leap-day starts, a start time daylight saving skips or repeats, the pack's 14 days across the September clock change).
- **Three worked examples** on Pro yearly at $240, started 20 September 2026:
  - Asked on 20 September, day 1: months 2 to 12 are unused, 11 whole months. Less one month's fee: 10 × $240 ÷ 12 = **$200**.
  - Asked on 15 January 2027, month 4: months 5 to 12 are unused, 8 whole months. Less one month's fee: 7 × $240 ÷ 12 = **$140** (the terms' own example).
  - Asked on 25 July 2027, month 11: only month 12 is unused. Less one month's fee: **$0**, there is nothing left to refund.
- **The quote route.** `GET /v1/admin/payments/charges/{charge}/quote` traces a charge to what it bought (its invoice's plan and period, or the Checkout Session's Move Pack and the move ledger) and answers `{ amount_cents, basis, explanation, what, remaining_cents, ends_plan, … }`. The amount never exceeds what Stripe says is left on the charge. A charge that traces to neither is "Not matched to a plan or pack" at $0.
- **The refund panel uses it.** The amount is pre-filled from the quote, and the panel shows the plan or pack and the policy's sentence. A different amount needs **Why a different amount?**. The refund POST now takes `policy_basis`, `quoted_cents`, `override_reason` and `end_plan`. The server re-quotes on confirm, refuses a quote that changed underneath the operator (409, with the new amount), and refuses a different amount with no reason (422). Migration 0107 adds `policy_basis`, `quoted_cents` and `override_reason` to `refund` (a CHECK requires the reason whenever amount and quote differ), so the record shows policy vs actual. A yearly refund ticks **End the plan today**: Stripe cancels the subscription now and the plan grant ends now, as the terms say. A Move Pack refunded in full takes its moves back out of the balance.
- **Ask for a refund.** Billing has an **Ask for a refund** button with the policy behind Explain. The seller picks a payment from the last 400 days, sees the policy's sentence and amount, and asks. The amount is always the server's quote. The operators get a mail (`email.refund_requested`). Payments lists **Refund requests**. **Approve** issues the amount quoted on the day they asked, emails the customer and, for a yearly plan, ends it today. **Decline** needs a reason, which the seller is emailed (`email.refund_declined`). Migration 0107 adds `refund_request` (org, charge, quoted amount, basis, status requested/approved/declined, note, who decided and when, the decline reason, the refund it issued). It is global like `refund`, and allows one open request per charge. New routes: `GET`/`POST /v1/billing/refund-requests`, `GET /v1/billing/payments/{charge}/refund-quote`, `POST /v1/admin/payments/refund-requests/{id}/approve` and `/decline`.
- **A cancelled-now subscription ends now.** `customer.subscription.deleted` now ends the grant at the subscription's `ended_at` when Stripe gives one. Before, it used the period end the subscription was cancelled out of, which would have kept a refunded yearly plan (or a deleted account's plan) entitled until the end of its year.
- Runbook: `docs/notes/runbooks/refunds.md` (rewritten: the policy as coded, how a charge is traced, the request flow, the mails).

### Deploy

- Migration 0107 runs on start.
- The Stripe key needs **write** on Subscriptions (an approved yearly refund cancels one) and **read** on Invoice Payments and Checkout Sessions (the quote's trace), besides what Payments already needed.
- Terms: the refunds section gains "Months are counted in New Zealand time from the day your yearly plan started." and points to Ask for a refund. This text is on the legal branch, which keeps the formula sentence word for word.
