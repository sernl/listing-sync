# Teachouse 0.21.0

## Pricing

The founder's 2026-10-05 review of the comparison table and the plan cards. Prices, price keys, the recommended plan (Pro) and grandfathering do not change.

| Row | Look | Starter | Pro | Studio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Edits sent to every marketplace | – | **–** (was daily) | **Weekly** (was every 6 hours) | **Daily** (was hourly) |
| Bulk actions (new row) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Schedule when a listing goes live | – | **–** (was ✓) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Term mapping and price rules | **–** (was ✓) | **–** (was ✓) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Statistics on every shop | – | **✓** (was –) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Catalogue size (was "Resources") | Catalogue up to 100 resources | Catalogue up to 250 resources | Catalogue up to 500 resources | Catalogue unlimited resources |
| Labels | **1** (was 5) | **5** (was unlimited) | **10** (was unlimited) | Unlimited |
| Templates / collections | 1 | 5 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Watermarked previews | 5 to try | 20 a month | 50 a month | **Unlimited** (was 100 a month) |

- **Term mapping and price rules start at Pro.** New capability `term_and_price_rules`. Below Pro, creating or editing a rule, fetching today's bank rate, previewing and approving or rejecting proposals answer 422 with `detail.feature = "term_and_price_rules"` and "Upgrade your plan to set up term mapping and price rules." Listing, reading a saved preview and deleting stay open, and rules and approvals already saved keep applying. The Target terms and Pricing pages show a banner and put the sentence on Save, the bank-rate button, Preview and every decision. On the Resources bulk bar, "Set target prices" and "Set licence and type" carry the same sentence below Pro; every other bulk verb runs on every plan.
- **Edits go out weekly on Pro and daily on Studio.** Starter no longer sends edits. The cadence control offers **Daily** and **Weekly**; six-hourly is gone because no plan reaches it. A shop stored at six hours is read at the plan's floor from the next pass, and the card opens on that floor.
- **Scheduling starts at Pro.** A Starter organisation's stored schedules stay listed and stop firing until it upgrades.
- **Statistics start at Starter.**
- **Labels climb 1 / 5 / 10 / unlimited**, like templates and collections. A seller already over a new ceiling keeps every label and can't add another until they upgrade or delete some.
- **Studio's watermarked previews are unlimited.** Each one is still counted, so the usage table keeps the figure.
- **The cards.** Look: 5 moves to try, Catalogue up to 100 resources, Bulk actions, Import from your shops or a spreadsheet, 5 watermarked previews to try, 1 template. Starter: 10 moves a month, Statistics on every shop, Catalogue up to 250 resources, 5 labels, 5 templates and 5 collections, 20 watermarked previews a month. Pro: 25 moves a month, Edits synced weekly, Schedule when a listing goes live, Automatic publishing rules, Catalogue up to 500 resources, 10 labels, templates and collections. Studio: 100 moves a month, Edits synced daily, Catalogue unlimited resources, Unlimited labels, templates and collections, Unlimited watermarked previews, Unlimited automations including scheduling, price rules and term mapping. A landing card now holds six lines. The Billing cards list everything a plan holds in the same words.
- **For developers:** `Capabilities::term_and_price_rules`, `FeatureKey::BulkActions`, `FeatureUnit::Catalogue` (`"catalogue"`); `PLAN_FEATURES` is 23 rows; `sync_settings::INTERVAL_CHOICES` is `[86_400, 604_800]`. `GET /v1/plans`, `GET /v1/entitlement` and the generated `plans.ts` / `plans.generated.js` / `vocab.ts` carry the new field and values. Details: pricing review §13, enforcement note §7.

Deploy: no migrations and no configuration. The new figures apply to every organisation on the next request. Run `just web-typegen` if a generated file is stale.
