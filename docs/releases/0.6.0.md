# Teachouse 0.6.0 for Windows and Android

This is the beta that follows 0.5.0.
It is still a beta, so expect rough edges, and please say what you find.
Two things stand out: you can put publishing on a timetable, and Teachouse can pull new resources from your shops on a cadence and publish them onward by a rule you set.
Under those, the Status page no longer breaks when you come back to it, and the app's "could not be reached" screen now says exactly what failed.
Almost all of this reaches you from the server, so a browser has it the moment it is published; 0.6.0 is the app build whose check-in serves the pulls the timetable asks for, so the Android and Windows apps need this version for sync pulls to run.

## What changed in the app

The app serves the pulls you schedule.
At every check-in the app asks whether a marketplace read is waiting, reads your shop when one is, and describes what you chose, exactly as it does when you start an import yourself.
It never starts a read on its own and never reads the same shop twice for one pull.

The "could not be reached" screen says what was thrown.
Under the sentence it prints the error's own name, whether the page came from the device's cache or the network, whether the device believed it was online, and what one plain request to Teachouse answered, so a screenshot of that screen tells us which layer failed.

## What changed in the console

Scheduling, under Automations, is live.
A schedule names a set of resources, everything with a label or the ones you tick, the marketplaces to send them to, Draft or Live, a time in your own timezone, and whether it repeats daily or weekly.
It can also republish a resource whenever you change it, on plans that include rules.
Each schedule shows its next and last run, and its Runs open a list of what each tick sent and what it skipped, with the reason, such as a listing already on its way.
Your own computer sends the work at its next check-in, as it does for everything else.

Marketplace Sync pulls new resources.
For each marketplace you have connected, switch on Pull new resources and choose every six hours, daily or weekly; your plan sets the shortest cadence it allows.
Teachouse asks your computer to read that shop on the timetable, skips every listing you already have, brings the rest in through Import with the duplicate review, and labels each with its source.
Publish new pulls to lets you name the marketplaces every new pull is published to, live, with your catalogue's own title and description.
The page lists every resource you have on more than one marketplace, and an activity log in plain words: what was pulled from where, what a schedule sent, and what a rule published.

The Status page no longer fails when you return to it.
Two pages shared one cache entry and stored it in different shapes, so opening Status after Settings or Marketplaces showed "This page could not be drawn".

## What changed on the site

Nothing on the front page changed in this release.

## Known limitations

A schedule that republishes on change cannot yet revise a live Tes listing; those resources are recorded as skipped with the reason until the Tes edit is captured.

New pulls are published with your catalogue's own words; per-marketplace wording from a template arrives with templates in the next release.

A scheduled pull or drop that finishes does not yet appear on the Notifications page; the activity log on Marketplace Sync and the schedule's runs are where to look.

Sync pulls need the 0.6.0 app on the computer or phone that holds the marketplace login; an older app will not serve them.

The service in front of Teachouse no longer offers HTTP/3, after an Android phone lost every request past the first page; if the app still cannot reach Teachouse, the new detail line on its screen is what we need to see.

Email about a finished run is not yet being sent.

There is no browser extension for Chrome or Firefox, and no Apple build.
No marketplace beyond Tes and TPT is connected.
The privacy and terms pages are still drafts.

## Updating

If you are on 0.5.0 on Windows, this arrives on its own.
The Android app does not update itself; download the package from this release and install it over the one you have.
The console needs no update at all.

## Installing it

Windows will warn you that the publisher is unknown.
That is expected for now: this build is not yet signed with a code-signing certificate, and the warning will go away in a later release.

## For operators

Migration 0071 creates `schedule`, `schedule_marketplace`, `schedule_product`, `schedule_run`, `marketplace_sync_setting`, `auto_publish_rule` and `auto_publish_run`, all under forced row-level security with no engine grant, and adds `import_run.scheduled`.
The tenant matrix grows to fifty-six tables.

`tam-server` hosts the scheduler pass every sixty seconds beside the import sweep; nothing changes in `tam-worker` or the deployment module.

Routes land under `/{version}/schedules`, `/{version}/sync/settings`, `/{version}/sync/activity`, `/{version}/sync/multi` and `GET /{version}/devices/{device}/import/open`.

The zone's HTTP/3 is off (`settings/http3`); both content-security policies carry a per-response nonce as of 0.5.0.
