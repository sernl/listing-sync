# Teachouse 0.16.0

## Guides

- **Every help guide is rewritten for the current console**: the three Marketplaces steps in their real order, the Stepper flows, Billing, **Files**, the rich-text description editor, the tour, and "device" wording throughout. Every screenshot is retaken from a seeded account ("Alice’s Classroom"); no real person's or shop's name appears in any guide.
- **New guide: Why you need the Teachouse app** (`/guides/why-the-app`). Marketplaces with no official API (TPT and Tes today) are connected in the free app on your own device, so Teachouse never holds your password and imported files stay on your device; everything else works in the browser.
- **Landing**: the FAQ answers "Why do I need the Teachouse app?" and "Do my files get uploaded to Teachouse?" with the 0.16 file facts, each linking to Marketplaces or the new guide, and the home page's app panel says the same in two sentences.
- **Contact**: `contact@teachouse.io` is the address in the footer, the maintenance page, the privacy policy and the terms (`supportEmail` in `apps/landing/src/site.js`). The privacy policy and the terms state where imported and uploaded files are kept.
- The stale guide copies under `web/src/*.md` are gone; `docs/guides/` is the only corpus.

Deploy, in this order:

1. `just guides-images https://teachouse.io <file holding an operator tam_session>` uploads every picture under `docs/guides/images/` and fails if a guide names a picture that is not there.
2. `tam-admin guides seed --dir docs/guides --db <url>` publishes the rewritten guides.
