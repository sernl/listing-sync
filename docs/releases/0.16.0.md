# Teachouse 0.16.0

## Seasons2

The marketing site has fourteen more seasonal themes, sixteen in all: New Year, Valentine's Day, St Patrick's Day, April Fools' Day, Easter, Matariki, Fourth of July, Back to school, Halloween, Guy Fawkes, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and Summer, Autumn, Winter and Spring.

- Each new theme has twelve hand-drawn stickers and a small mark for the console's top bar. For example, New Year has a clock holding books, a party popper, a cake and fireworks, with a skyline of Auckland, Sydney, London, New York and Tokyo along the top of the footer. Matariki has a kererū holding books, a ruru, tūī, a manu tukutuku kite, a hāngī and kete, harakeke, and the Matariki star cluster rising over hills at dawn.
- Every theme hangs a garland under the header and puts a striped ribbon along its lower edge. It tints the hero, dresses the top corners, and places stickers in the space above and under the hero art, beside the section headings and in the footer's corner. The ribbon and the tint use the site's palette, so they follow light and dark.
- A phone gets a lighter set: the ribbon, the garland, one small corner and the theme's character standing on the footer's edge.
- Stickers never sit over text or buttons and never take a click. Each theme has one small idle motion: a heart that beats, a snowflake that turns, a leaf that sways, a kite that sways. The motion is off for anyone whose device asks for reduced motion.
- The garland now belongs to the page rather than the sticky header, so it scrolls away instead of riding over the text.
- **Admin, Site:** the theme picker shows all sixteen themes with their console marks and when each usually runs. Each theme has its own Preview link, which opens the home page with `?season=<name>`.
- Preview any theme on any page with `?season=<name>`, for example `?season=new-year` or `?season=st-patricks`.

### Matariki: what the stickers draw on

The Matariki stickers use everyday motifs, not sacred ones. The motifs are the star cluster rising before dawn, kites, harakeke, the hāngī, kete, kūmara and native birds. There is no tā moko, whakairo, kōwhaiwhai, hei-tiki or Māori text. The nine-star cluster and what each star stands for follow Te Papa's [The stars of Matariki](https://www.tepapa.govt.nz/digital-museum/explore-digital-museum/matariki-maori-new-year/what-and-who-matariki/stars-matariki) and [Are there seven or nine stars?](https://www.tepapa.govt.nz/discover-collections/read-watch-play/maori/matariki-maori-new-year/matariki-star-cluster-are-there), which draw on Rangi Matamua's *Matariki: The Star of the Year* (Huia, 2017). Kites follow the NZASE [Manu tukutuku](https://nzase.org.nz/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/2022-09-Manu-tukutuku-NZASE-resource-1.pdf) resource: manu were flown to mark the start of Matariki.

### Deploy

No migration. Themes are stored by name in `site_setting`, and Halloween and Christmas keep their names, so any theme already saved still reads.

## Feedback

- **Connecting TPT or TES works on Windows.** The app used to put the whole marketplace login in Windows Credential Manager, which refuses anything longer than 2560 characters, so connecting failed with "the session store refused: Attribute password encoded as UTF-16 is longer than platform limit of 2560 chars". Every platform now keeps the login sealed (XChaCha20-Poly1305) in a file in the app's own data folder, and the operating system's credential store holds only the 32-byte key that opens it. A login an earlier version kept in the credential store moves into the file the first time the app reads it, so nobody is signed out by the update.
- **The tour ends on getting the app.** In a browser, the last card is **Start here: download the app**, with a button to the Get-the-app step on Marketplaces. In the app it still ends on **Create your first resource**.
- **A short tour the first time you open the app.** Three cards on each new device: marketplaces are connected here, in the app; open Marketplaces and press Connect; importing, distributing and managing work in the app or in your browser. It is shown once per device.
- **Catalogue → Files.** The Catalogue tab "Your devices' files" is now called **Files**, in the navigation, the page title and the guides.

## PerfLogin

The sign-in, sign-up and password-reset pages now open in about a second on a slow connection, down from about three.

- The signed-out pages have their own light frame: none of the console's shell, query client, icon-heavy chrome, analytics or compiled rules load before the form, and nothing waits for the API before the first paint. Maintenance mode is checked right after that paint.
- Product analytics loads only after the page has finished loading and gone idle. On the signed-out pages it also waits for your first click or key press. The sign-in library loads when you submit.
- The fonts are content-hashed and cached for a year. The two used on the sign-in card are preloaded, and each sign-in page's own scripts are preloaded from the first byte, together with a connection to the security check.
- The PDF library behind the preview maker now loads only when you make a preview. The console's rules module now waits until the resource list has finished loading.

Measured with headless Chromium 153 on a cold cache, throttled to 1.5 Mbps down and 150 ms round trip. Each figure is the median of 5 runs. Prod is teachouse.io on 0.15.0. Local runs go through HTTP/2 with brotli, as prod does.

|---|---|---|

| `/login` form typeable | 3.3 s (prod) | 1.16 s |
