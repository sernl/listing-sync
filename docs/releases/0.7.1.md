# Teachouse 0.7.1 for Windows and Android

This is a fix release that follows 0.7.0, for the four things you told us about after installing it.

## What changed in the app

A machine you signed out from the console can be signed back in.
Signing a machine out under Your machines used to be permanent: the app kept its identity, was told it was signed out at every check-in, forgot every marketplace login it captured, and every Connect ended in "could not be saved on this device".
Now the console shows one banner on that machine, "This machine was signed out from the console on <date>", with a Sign this machine back in button; pressing it restores the machine and Connect works again.
Pressing Connect on a machine that is still signed out tells you so before it opens anything, instead of opening the marketplace's sign-in and failing afterwards.

On Android the bottom bar sits above the phone's own buttons.
On a phone with three-button navigation the app's Import, Catalogue, New, Automate and Markets sat underneath Android's back, home and recents buttons, so neither row could be pressed cleanly; the app now ends where the buttons begin.

The app no longer fails to start after we publish an update to the console.
The console's page was kept by the app past an update and then asked for files the update had replaced, which showed "could not load" until the app was closed and reopened; the page is now checked for freshness on every load.

## What changed in the console

Every screen says which machine you are on.
Account, and the account button at the top, say "You are on <machine> in the Teachouse app" or "You are in a browser, not the Teachouse app".
Under Your machines, the app's own machine is marked This machine, and each machine reads, in this order: signed out from the console on a date, connected and checked in a moment ago, or last seen at a time.

When a page cannot be drawn, the message says what went wrong, in small type under the sentence, so a screenshot tells us.

## Known limitations

As for 0.7.0.

## Updating

If you are on 0.7.0 on Windows, this arrives on its own.
The Android app does not update itself; download the package from this release and install it over the one you have.
The console needs no update at all.

## Installing it

Windows will warn you that the publisher is unknown; that is expected for now.

## For operators

`POST /{version}/devices/{device}/restore` clears a device's revocation for a signed-in seller at that machine, within the plan's device allowance.
The console shell is served `Cache-Control: no-cache` and `/_app/immutable/` a year immutable.
The Android activity pads its content by the system-bar and keyboard insets.
