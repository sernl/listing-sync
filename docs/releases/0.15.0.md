# Teachouse 0.15.0

## Privacy

Teachouse now has a real privacy policy at `/privacy/`, replacing the placeholder.
It says what we collect, why, where it is kept (New Zealand, with analytics in the EU), who else sees it, how long we keep it, and how to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion.
It is written against how Teachouse actually works: marketplace logins and imported files stay on your devices, card details stay with Stripe, and analytics are tied to your organisation rather than to you.

The sign-up and sign-in pages now say that continuing means agreeing to the Terms and the Privacy Policy, with links to both, and the Terms page links to the Privacy Policy.

Still to do before the policy is complete: a monitored privacy contact address (the policy says one is being set up until `supportEmail` is set in `apps/landing/src/site.js`).

## UIPolish

- **Add to a collection** is rebuilt: a title and one line under it, then one row per collection with its name and how many resources it holds, and **A new collection** last. Choosing the new one opens its name (and an optional description) right under that row. The dialog is 420–480px wide on a computer and a full-width sheet on a phone, and nothing overlaps or wraps word by word any more.

A dialog that is part-way through saving stays open, as it already did for Esc. Starting a text selection inside a dialog and letting go outside it no longer closes it.

- On Resources, the three marketplace filter chips are round and the same height as the chips beside them. The coloured dot in front of "Tes signed out" at the top of the page now lines up with the middle of the words.

## RequestMail

A seller's marketplace request now reaches a person.

- The request form says what happens: "We email the Teachouse team; they reply to <your email>." The confirmation repeats where the reply will go.
- One person may send 3 requests in any rolling 24 hours. The 4th is refused with "You’ve sent 3 requests today. Try again tomorrow." (HTTP 429), stores nothing and mails nobody. The per-organisation cap of 20 is unchanged.
- The operators' mail is titled "Marketplace request: <marketplace>" and states the requester's name and email, their organisation, the marketplace, their note and a button to the organisation in admin. Replying to it answers the seller (the mail's reply-to is their address, when the identity service has verified it).
- Each operator's address is read from the identity service before anything is sent. If that call fails, the log line and the outbox's recorded error name the subject, and the outbox retries with its usual backoff.
- New optional `--ops-email <address>` (NixOS: `services.teachouse.server.mail.opsEmail`): when no operator has a verified address, the mail goes to this inbox instead. It is not copied when an operator is reached. The flag is refused without the rest of the mail flags.

## Admin users

The Admin **Users** page is a table you can page through and act on in bulk.

- **A real table.** Columns are Name and email, Organisation, Plan, Status, Joined and Last sign-in, with dividers, zebra rows and a hover. Click **Joined** to flip newest-first and oldest-first across every account. Click **Last sign-in** to sort the page you are on. On a phone each account is a small card, with **Select page** and a **Sort** menu above the list.
- **Pages of 25.** The footer says "26–50 of 240 accounts" and has Previous and Next. The search box and the All / Unverified / Banned / Operators / No app user chips are unchanged. The chips count the page on screen.
- **Bulk select.** Every row has a tick box, and the header one ticks the whole page. Once a page is ticked, **Select all 240** takes every account that matches the search, on every page. The bar that appears offers Make operator, Remove operator, Make identity admin, Make ordinary user, Unban, Ban and Delete. Each one asks first and names the count. Delete also asks you to type the count. Accounts are done one at a time with a running tally, and the result lists every account that failed, with the reason.
- **The account sheet** closes when you press Escape or click outside it. Its new **Access** section has two switches, each with a "Why?" note:
  - **Operator** opens the Admin area and every organisation. You can turn it on or off for anyone who has opened the app. You cannot turn off your own, so there is always someone left who can turn it back on.
  - **Identity admin** is the sign-in service's role that can ban, sign in as and delete accounts. It replaces the old Role menu.
- An operator can no longer be deleted until **Operator** is off. The refusal now says so, where it used to point at `tam-admin`.

Under the hood:

- `GET /v1/admin/users` is paged: `?cursor=&limit=` (1–100, default 25) answers `{ users, total, next_cursor? }`. The cursor is keyset on the account's creation time and id, so a sign-up while you page cannot show one person twice. `api.adminUsers()` in the console walks the pages, so the Overview, Organisations and Impersonations pages still see everyone.
- New routes `POST` and `DELETE /v1/admin/operators/{subject}` grant and withdraw the operator marking. Both are for operators only. Each answers 404 for an identity with no app user, and `DELETE` answers 409 `refusal: "self"` for your own marking. Granting someone who already has it writes nothing.
- Every grant and withdrawal is recorded in the new `platform_operator_event` table (migration **0096**), in the same transaction as the change, with who made it: `operator:<uuid>` from the console, or the `--by` name from `tam-admin`. `tam-admin revoke` now takes `--by <who>` too, and defaults to `tam-admin`. The table has no RLS and the backoffice role gets no grant on it, like `platform_operator`.
- `web/src/lib/Sheet.svelte` is a reusable side sheet (bottom sheet on a phone) built on `lightDismiss`.

## Files anywhere

Imported files can now be opened from any browser the seller signs in to.

- **Teachouse keeps a copy of each imported file.** An import still leaves the original on the device that read it. The Teachouse app on that device then copies each original to Teachouse in the background: on start, on every check-in, and as soon as an import keeps a new file. It copies only files a live resource uses, smallest first, one at a time, and never two runs at once.
- **Within the plan's storage.** Copies count towards the plan's storage like any upload. When a copy would not fit, the file stays on the device and the console says "Your plan’s storage is full — files stay on your device." A single copy is capped at 96 MB, the same as an upload: Cloudflare refuses a larger request at the edge, and a sealed file is sealed whole in memory, so a bigger copy is neither reachable nor safe yet. A larger file stays on the device and the console says so.
- **View streams.** The resource's Files list opens PDFs and pictures from Teachouse's copy, a range at a time, so the first pages of a 30 MB PDF draw before the rest arrives. The file route (`GET /v1/products/{p}/files/{f}/content`) answers `Range` requests (`206`, `416`) and `?download=1` as an attachment. A sealed file still opens whole on the server; files above 256 MB are refused rather than opened.
- **Download.** Every file in a resource's Files list — the file, the preview and the thumbnail — has a **Download** button, and so does every file Teachouse holds in **Your devices' files**.
- **Make a preview from your file** reads Teachouse's copy, so it works in any browser. Until the copy exists the form says "This file is on your device only. Open the Teachouse app on that device to copy it to Teachouse." with a **Why?** beside it.
- **Your devices' files** has a **Where** column: this device only, your device and Teachouse, or Teachouse only.
- **Imported files have names.** An imported file shows the name the device recorded for it instead of "unnamed file". A single-file TPT product, which the marketplace hands over without a name, is now named after its listing (`Full Unit Whole Numbers.pdf`), cut so the whole name with its extension stays within the 200 bytes a file name may carry. Files imported before this release that were filed under the marketplace's number show, open and download under the resource's title instead; **Your devices' files** still lists them by the number.

New routes: `GET /v1/library/missing`, `PUT /v1/library/files/{hash}`, `GET /v1/library/files/{hash}/content`. No migration.

## Android

- The Android download is now one universal file, `Teachouse_<version>_universal.apk`, carrying arm64 and x86_64 code, so the same download installs on phones, tablets, Chromebooks and emulators. 32-bit-only devices remain unsupported.
- The app declares every screen size, tablets included, and requires no phone-only hardware.
- The release log prints the signing certificate's SHA-256 digest, the value to compare when a device says "App not installed".
- The Android runbook explains each "App not installed" message and `adb install` error, and when uninstalling first is the only way onto a release: a device that ever had a debug build.
- The app no longer sits on a blank white page when Teachouse does not load. If the page has not appeared within 8 seconds, or the device's browser engine reports that it could not load it, the app goes back to its own start card, which says "Teachouse can’t be reached. Check your connection, then try again." with a Try again button and the technical detail behind "Why?". The same card answers a launch with no connection at all. Computers get the same behaviour.

## Import mapping

Resources imported from TPT now arrive with TPT's own answers filled in, and a listing that is already live on TPT is no longer refused for fields TPT already holds.

- **Subject Area, Tags and Format are filled.** The import sorts each of the listing's TPT tags onto the picker that offers it on TPT's form (Subject Area; Tag, which is TPT's theme, audience and language groups; Format), using the committed TPT vocabulary. Tags with no box on the form — resource types such as Worksheets or Unit Plans, supports, programmes — are shown read-only in the TPT panel as **Also on your TPT listing**, and every update to TPT sends them back unchanged.
- **Tax code, copyright statement, localisation and four pictures.** The Teachouse app now reads these from TPT too. The copyright statement is TPT's own (`ORIGINAL_WORK` → "my own original work", `USED_COPYRIGHTED_MATERIALS` → the second statement); it is never defaulted. The app fetches the listing's four pictures (full size, else large) only from `https://ecdn.teacherspayteachers.com/thumbitem/…`, without the seller's TPT login, and they fill the four Thumbnails slots.
- **Imports never overwrite the seller.** An import, a re-import and a refresh fill only fields the seller left empty; a missing licence is filled the same way. Pressing Refresh twice changes nothing the second time.
- **Live listings are not refused for what TPT already holds.** Empty-field refusals (Subject Area, Tag, copyright statement, tax code, blank price) apply only when the resource is going to TPT and is not already bound and live there. A value TPT would refuse — too many choices, a price under TPT's minimum — is still refused, because the next update would send it. A resource going only to Tes is not held to TPT's form. A marketplace where the resource is already live is not asked for a licence.
- **Updating a TPT listing keeps its tax code and shelves** and posts back its Subject Area, Tag and Format picks and the tags that have no box.
- **Refresh from TPT.** A finished TPT import's page has a **Refresh from TPT** button. It fills Subject Area, Tags and Format from what that import saved, without the app, and says how many resources it filled, how many had nothing new, and how many still need their pictures, tax code and copyright.

New route: `POST /v1/imports/runs/{run}/refresh` → `{ filled, unchanged, read_again }`. No migration. New desktop app release required for pictures, tax code, copyright statement and localisation.

### Backfilling the founder's 154 products

Imports made before this release saved each listing's TPT tags but not its tax code, copyright statement, localisation or pictures.

1. Deploy the server and console, and publish the new Teachouse app.
2. For each finished TPT import in **Import** (the history list), open it and press **Refresh from TPT**. Subject Area, Tags and Format fill in at once for every resource that import made. The line under the button says how many resources still need a fresh read: before step 3 that should be all of them (154 across the imports).
3. On the device with the TPT connection, update the Teachouse app, open **Import**, choose TPT and press **Import from TPT**, then **Select all** and import. Resources that already have a copyright statement are skipped as before; the ones without one are read again, which downloads each file once more and fills tax code, copyright statement, localisation and the four pictures. Nothing the seller already set is changed.
4. Press **Refresh from TPT** on any of the imports again. The last count should read `0 resources still need…`; any resource still counted there is one TPT did not return (deleted or taken down on TPT) and needs its details entered by hand.
5. Spot-check a few resources: Subject Area, Tag, Format, Tax Code and copyright statement are set, four Thumbnails show, and the page shows no red refusals.

Not fixed in this release: updating a listing on TPT still sends teaching duration and answer key as fixed values, which clears them on TPT.
