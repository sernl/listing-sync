# Teachouse 0.15.0

## Admin users

The Admin **Users** page is a table you can page through and act on in bulk.

- **A real table.** Columns are Name and email, Organisation, Plan, Status, Joined and Last sign-in, with dividers, zebra rows and a hover. Click **Joined** to flip newest-first and oldest-first across every account. Click **Last sign-in** to sort the page you are on. On a phone each account is a small card, with **Select page** and a **Sort** menu above the list.
- **Pages of 25.** The footer says "26–50 of 240 accounts" and has Previous and Next. The search box and the All / Unverified / Banned / Operators / No app user chips are unchanged. The chips count the page on screen.
- **Bulk select.** Every row has a tick box, and the header one ticks the whole page. Once a page is ticked, **Select all 240** takes every account that matches the search, on every page. The bar that appears offers Make operator, Remove operator, Make identity admin, Make ordinary user, Unban, Ban and Delete. Each one asks first and names the count. Delete also asks you to type the count. Accounts are done one at a time with a running tally, and the result lists every account that failed, with the reason.
- **The account sheet** closes when you press Escape or click outside it. Its new **Access** section has two switches, each with a "Why?" note:
  - **Operator** opens the Admin area and every organisation. You can turn it on or off for anyone who has opened the app. You cannot turn off your own, so there is always someone left who can turn it back on.
  - **Identity admin** is the sign-in service's role that can ban, sign in as and delete accounts. It replaces the old Role menu.
- An operator can no longer be deleted until **Operator** is off. The refusal now says so, where it used to point at `tam-admin`.

Under the hood:

- `GET /v1/admin/users` is paged: `?cursor=&limit=` (1–100, default 25) answers `{ users, total, next_cursor? }`. The cursor is keyset on the account's creation time and id, so a sign-up while you page cannot show one person twice. `api.adminUsers()` in the console walks the pages, so the Overview, Organisations and Impersonations pages still see everyone.
- New routes `POST` and `DELETE /v1/admin/operators/{subject}` grant and withdraw the operator marking. Both are for operators only. Each answers 404 for an identity with no app user, and `DELETE` answers 409 `refusal: "self"` for your own marking. Granting someone who already has it writes nothing.
- Every grant and withdrawal is recorded in the new `platform_operator_event` table (migration **0096**), in the same transaction as the change, with who made it: `operator:<uuid>` from the console, or the `--by` name from `tam-admin`. `tam-admin revoke` now takes `--by <who>` too, and defaults to `tam-admin`. The table has no RLS and the backoffice role gets no grant on it, like `platform_operator`.
- `web/src/lib/Sheet.svelte` is a reusable side sheet (bottom sheet on a phone) built on `lightDismiss`.
