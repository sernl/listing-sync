# 0.15.0

## BillingAdminUI

- **Billing page, calmer.** Your plan now sits in one card at the top: its name and one-line summary, the price you pay, when it renews or ends, and the Adjust plan and Cancel plan buttons. Cancelling still asks you to confirm first.
- Payment and Your moves sit side by side. The card shows its brand, last four digits and expiry, and how moves work is behind "How it works".
- Invoices are a proper table (Date, Description, Amount, Status, Receipt). Amounts line up on the right, and on a phone each invoice becomes a small card.
- Plans: the Monthly/Yearly switch sits beside the heading. Every card is the same height. The card for your plan is marked, and the recommended plan is raised with a badge once the plan table marks one. Card lines come from the plan table.
- **Admin Pricing, top down.** Four numbered sections: Running now, Scheduled, Codes (a table with an End button on each code), then Create. Create is one form card. Sale, one-off discount and discount code are a switch at the top, the fields are in two columns, the first and last day sit side by side, and a line at the foot says what will run (for example "25% off every plan, 1–31 Oct, banner …"). A field that is wrong says so under itself, in place of its hint. Finished discounts are folded away at the bottom.
- Shared table styles live in `web/src/lib/styles/data.css` (`.data-table`, `.data-table-wrap`, `.data-empty`, `.segmented`) for other admin pages to use.
