# Teachouse 0.15.0

## Pricing

The pricing page is now a plain good, better, best: four cards, one comparison table and the Move Packs under them. The reasoning behind every price and cap is in `docs/notes/design/research/2026-09-29-pricing-structure-review.md`.

- **Prices are unchanged.** Starter is $12 a month or $96 a year, Sync $29 or $240, and Studio $59 or $480. The Move Packs keep their prices too, and no Stripe price changes.
- **A Monthly / Yearly switch** sits above the cards, set to yearly. Each paid card shows "$20 a month" with "Billed yearly, $240 · save $108 a year" under it. Monthly shows "Billed monthly, cancel any time", and each card's button opens the checkout for the period shown. The switch is two ordinary radio buttons, so it works from the keyboard and with a screen reader, and needs no script.
- **Sync is marked Recommended.** Its card is raised with a badge, and its column is tinted in the table.
- **Each card is short:** who the plan is for in one sentence, the price, a button, and at most five lines. The paid cards list only what they add: "Everything in Starter, plus: …".
- **"Compare the plans"** is a table under the cards. Features are the rows, grouped into Moves and updates, Automations, Your catalogue, Statistics and AI, and Help. The plans are the columns, and each cell shows a tick, a dash or a figure. On a phone the table scrolls sideways and the feature names stay pinned on the left. The AI description fill shows a hollow mark and "Coming soon", never a tick.
- **During a sale** the banner and struck prices work as before. The "save $N a year" line is hidden while a sale is on, because it was worked out at list price.
- **The questions are cut from eleven to eight.** A new one covers running out of moves (nothing is charged by surprise). The 90-day free re-edit is no longer promised, because nothing in the product does it yet.
- **Plan changes, both upward:**
  - Look now includes 1 collection (it had none).
  - Labels are unlimited on every paid plan (they were 20, 20 and 50). Look keeps 5.
- **For developers:**
  - `tam-limits` now holds the comparison table as data: `PLAN_FEATURES`, `PLAN_FEATURE_GROUPS`, `FeatureKey`, `FeatureGroup`, `FeatureUnit`, `Included`, `PerPlan`.
  - Each plan row now carries `recommended` and `tagline`.
  - `GET /v1/plans` and the generated `plans.ts` / `plans.generated.js` include the new fields.
