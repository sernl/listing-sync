# Teachouse 0.15.0

## Privacy

Teachouse now has a real privacy policy at `/privacy/`, replacing the placeholder.
It says what we collect, why, where it is kept (New Zealand, with analytics in the EU), who else sees it, how long we keep it, and how to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion.
It is written against how Teachouse actually works: marketplace logins and imported files stay on your devices, card details stay with Stripe, and analytics are tied to your organisation rather than to you.

The sign-up and sign-in pages now say that continuing means agreeing to the Terms and the Privacy Policy, with links to both, and the Terms page links to the Privacy Policy.

Still to do before the policy is complete: a monitored privacy contact address (the policy says one is being set up until `supportEmail` is set in `apps/landing/src/site.js`).

## UIPolish

- **Add to a collection** is rebuilt: a title and one line under it, then one row per collection with its name and how many resources it holds, and **A new collection** last. Choosing the new one opens its name (and an optional description) right under that row. The dialog is 420–480px wide on a computer and a full-width sheet on a phone, and nothing overlaps or wraps word by word any more.
- Every dialog closes when you click or tap outside it, as well as with Esc: the **Why?** and **How it works** notes, the file viewer, the publish, cross-list, label, template, delete and cancel-plan dialogs, and the admin sign-ins and delete dialogs. A dialog that is part-way through saving stays open, as it already did for Esc. Starting a text selection inside a dialog and letting go outside it no longer closes it.
- Page headers line up: the round help button, the page's own buttons and any status pill sit on one line level with the page icon, on a computer and on a phone.
- On Resources, the three marketplace filter chips are round and the same height as the chips beside them. The coloured dot in front of "Tes signed out" at the top of the page now lines up with the middle of the words.

## RequestMail

A seller's marketplace request now reaches a person.

- The request form says what happens: "We email the Teachouse team; they reply to <your email>." The confirmation repeats where the reply will go.
- One person may send 3 requests in any rolling 24 hours. The 4th is refused with "You’ve sent 3 requests today. Try again tomorrow." (HTTP 429), stores nothing and mails nobody. The per-organisation cap of 20 is unchanged.
- The operators' mail is titled "Marketplace request: <marketplace>" and states the requester's name and email, their organisation, the marketplace, their note and a button to the organisation in admin. Replying to it answers the seller (the mail's reply-to is their address, when the identity service has verified it).
- Each operator's address is read from the identity service before anything is sent. If that call fails, the log line and the outbox's recorded error name the subject, and the outbox retries with its usual backoff.
- New optional `--ops-email <address>` (NixOS: `services.teachouse.server.mail.opsEmail`): when no operator has a verified address, the mail goes to this inbox instead. It is not copied when an operator is reached. The flag is refused without the rest of the mail flags.

## Admin users

The Admin **Users** page is a table you can page through and act on in bulk.

- **A real table.** Columns are Name and email, Organisation, Plan, Status, Joined and Last sign-in, with dividers, zebra rows and a hover. Click **Joined** to flip newest-first and oldest-first across every account. Click **Last sign-in** to sort the page you are on. On a phone each account is a small card, with **Select page** and a **Sort** menu above the list.
- **Pages of 25.** The footer says "26–50 of 240 accounts" and has Previous and Next. The search box and the All / Unverified / Banned / Operators / No app user chips are unchanged. The chips count the page on screen.
- **Bulk select.** Every row has a tick box, and the header one ticks the whole page. Once a page is ticked, **Select all 240** takes every account that matches the search, on every page. The bar that appears offers Make operator, Remove operator, Make identity admin, Make ordinary user, Unban, Ban and Delete. Each one asks first and names the count. Delete also asks you to type the count. Accounts are done one at a time with a running tally, and the result lists every account that failed, with the reason.
- **The account sheet** closes when you press Escape or click outside it. Its new **Access** section has two switches, each with a "Why?" note:
  - **Operator** opens the Admin area and every organisation. You can turn it on or off for anyone who has opened the app. You cannot turn off your own, so there is always someone left who can turn it back on.
  - **Identity admin** is the sign-in service's role that can ban, sign in as and delete accounts. It replaces the old Role menu.
- An operator can no longer be deleted until **Operator** is off. The refusal now says so, where it used to point at `tam-admin`.

Under the hood:

- `GET /v1/admin/users` is paged: `?cursor=&limit=` (1–100, default 25) answers `{ users, total, next_cursor? }`. The cursor is keyset on the account's creation time and id, so a sign-up while you page cannot show one person twice. `api.adminUsers()` in the console walks the pages, so the Overview, Organisations and Impersonations pages still see everyone.
- New routes `POST` and `DELETE /v1/admin/operators/{subject}` grant and withdraw the operator marking. Both are for operators only. Each answers 404 for an identity with no app user, and `DELETE` answers 409 `refusal: "self"` for your own marking. Granting someone who already has it writes nothing.
- Every grant and withdrawal is recorded in the new `platform_operator_event` table (migration **0096**), in the same transaction as the change, with who made it: `operator:<uuid>` from the console, or the `--by` name from `tam-admin`. `tam-admin revoke` now takes `--by <who>` too, and defaults to `tam-admin`. The table has no RLS and the backoffice role gets no grant on it, like `platform_operator`.
- `web/src/lib/Sheet.svelte` is a reusable side sheet (bottom sheet on a phone) built on `lightDismiss`.

## Pricing

The pricing page is now a plain good, better, best: four cards, one comparison table and the Move Packs under them. The reasoning behind every price and cap is in `docs/notes/design/research/2026-09-29-pricing-structure-review.md`.

- **Prices are unchanged.** Starter is $12 a month or $96 a year, Sync $29 or $240, and Studio $59 or $480. The Move Packs keep their prices too, and no Stripe price changes.
- **A Monthly / Yearly switch** sits above the cards, set to yearly. Each paid card shows "$20 a month" with "Billed yearly, $240 · save $108 a year" under it. Monthly shows "Billed monthly, cancel any time", and each card's button opens the checkout for the period shown. The switch is two ordinary radio buttons, so it works from the keyboard and with a screen reader, and needs no script.
- **Sync is marked Recommended.** Its card is raised with a badge, and its column is tinted in the table.
- **Each card is short:** who the plan is for in one sentence, the price, a button, and at most five lines. The paid cards list only what they add: "Everything in Starter, plus: …".
- **"Compare the plans"** is a table under the cards. Features are the rows, grouped into Moves and updates, Automations, Your catalogue, Statistics and AI, and Help. The plans are the columns, and each cell shows a tick, a dash or a figure. On a phone the table scrolls sideways and the feature names stay pinned on the left. The AI description fill shows a hollow mark and "Coming soon", never a tick.
- **During a sale** the banner and struck prices work as before. The "save $N a year" line is hidden while a sale is on, because it was worked out at list price.
- **The questions are cut from eleven to eight.** A new one covers running out of moves (nothing is charged by surprise). The 90-day free re-edit is no longer promised, because nothing in the product does it yet.
- **Plan changes, both upward:**
  - Look now includes 1 collection (it had none).
  - Labels are unlimited on every paid plan (they were 20, 20 and 50). Look keeps 5.
- **For developers:**
  - `tam-limits` now holds the comparison table as data: `PLAN_FEATURES`, `PLAN_FEATURE_GROUPS`, `FeatureKey`, `FeatureGroup`, `FeatureUnit`, `Included`, `PerPlan`.
  - Each plan row now carries `recommended` and `tagline`.
  - `GET /v1/plans` and the generated `plans.ts` / `plans.generated.js` include the new fields.
