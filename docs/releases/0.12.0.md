# Teachouse 0.12.0

Thumbnails are drawn from the file: a PDF's first page is rendered on the device (and on the server for replacements) at 1600×1200 instead of a flat red card, stored document previews are never enlarged, and a bundle's first PDF is rendered when its own preview is too small. The desktop's import re-draws a card thumbnail on the next import; `tam-admin covers repair` redraws the ones the server holds.

The five Automations pages (Schedules, Migrations, Pricing, Target terms, Updates) are guided flows: numbered steps, a diagram of source → rule → target with a worked example, one sentence per instruction, the long explanations behind "Why?" and "Read the fine print", the primary action beside the step on desktop and in a sticky bar on phones. Every other console page, the guides, the server's messages and the landing site are reworded for teachers; the pricing cards carry the founder's wording (Look is a trial, Sync a subscription, Move Packs one-off).

The console opens in light mode unless the seller chose otherwise, and dark mode is charcoal rather than indigo; the landing's dark palette matches. Landing: larger centred Import/Distribute/Manage steps, a definition of a move, fresh Resources screenshots.

Limits from the 2026-09-26 pricing re-evaluation: Look keeps 256 MiB of covers, free moves are five per account once, uploads are capped at 96 MiB, one upload in flight on Look and three on paid plans, and the fleet breaker only halts a marketplace when at least three accounts fail together. Prices are unchanged.

Desktop wire: import covers may be up to 2 MiB.
