# Teachouse 0.14.0

## Billing

"Plan and moves" is now **Billing**, at `/settings/billing`. The old address still works and forwards there, including the `?checkout=` Stripe adds on the way back.

- The top of the page shows the plan you hold in its own colour, with what it gives you in one line and when it renews. **Adjust plan** takes you to the plans further down.
- **Payment** shows the card you pay with (brand and last four digits). **Update** opens Stripe's own card page and brings you back.
- **Invoices** lists your recent bills with the date, total, status and a **View** link to Stripe's invoice page, where you can download a PDF.
- **Cancellation**: **Cancel plan** asks you to confirm, then stops the plan from renewing. You keep the plan until the end of the period you paid for, and the page tells you that date. **Keep my plan** undoes it until then. A cancellation made in Stripe's portal shows up here too.
- After a checkout, the banner now says your moves are on the card **below**, which is where they are.

### Under the hood

- Migration `0089_billing_invoices.sql` adds `billing_subscription.cancel_at_period_end` (default `false`). The `customer.subscription.updated` webhook records it, and treats a scheduled `cancel_at` as a cancellation too. An invoice or checkout event leaves the stored value alone, so an invoice can't quietly renew a plan the seller cancelled. A new subscription starts with it cleared.
- `GET /v1/billing` adds `cancel_at_period_end` and `ends_at`. While the plan runs, the response has either `renews_at` or `ends_at`, never both.
- New routes: `GET /v1/billing/invoices` (read from Stripe on each request, drafts left out, nothing stored), `GET /v1/billing/payment-method` (the subscription's card, or the customer's default card if the subscription has none), `POST /v1/billing/payment-method` (a Billing Portal session with `flow_data[type]=payment_method_update`), and `POST /v1/billing/cancel` and `POST /v1/billing/resume` (`cancel_at_period_end` on the Stripe subscription; each returns the updated billing read).
- Checkout and the portal now send the browser back to `/settings/billing`. The landing page's links to `/settings/subscription` get forwarded there.

### Upgrading

Server, then console. Run the migration before the server starts. In the Stripe dashboard, the Billing Portal needs **payment method update** turned on (Account → Billing → Customer portal) for **Update** to open.
## Tiers

Teachouse now sells three subscription plans above the free one, each with a monthly and a yearly price. The founding offer and the paid "Move with me" session are no longer sold.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Look | Free | Free | 5, once |
| Starter | $12 | $96 | 10 a month |
| Sync | $29 | $240 | 25 a month |
| Studio | $59 | $480 | 100 a month |

- Sync is unchanged. Existing Sync subscribers keep their plan, their price and their moves.
- Starter is new. It keeps edits in step once a day and includes scheduling, 5 templates and 5 collections. Statistics and automatic publishing rules start at Sync.
- Studio is now on sale. It includes 100 moves a month, hourly updates, unlimited templates and collections, and priority support.
- Move Packs are unchanged and can be bought on any plan.
- The pricing page and the console's Billing page show four plan cards. The Billing page has a monthly/yearly switch and marks the plan you are on. Someone who already pays changes plan from **Manage billing**.
- Plan card wording follows the founder's review. The first lines are "Import from wherever you sell" and "Edit once, sync everywhere", every card has "Add a watermarked preview of your file", AI reads "AI description fill, coming soon", and the free plan's trial moves are listed last. The labels are lowercase: (trial), (subscription), (one-off).

### Under the hood

- `tam-limits`: `Plan` gains `starter` (strength between Free and Sync). `PriceKey` is now `starter_{monthly,yearly}`, `sync_{monthly,yearly}`, `studio_{monthly,yearly}` and `pack_{20,50,100,250,500}`. `founding_yearly`, `move_with_me`, `FOUNDING` and `SERVICES` are gone. New: `PriceKey::plan()` and `PriceKey::list_cents()`. `PlanRow` carries `monthly_key`/`yearly_key` and drops `sold`.
- `GET /v1/plans` no longer returns `services` or `founding`. `GET /v1/billing` no longer returns `founding`.
- Checkout grants the plan the price key sells. A price change on a live subscription (a switch in the Stripe portal) revokes the old grant and writes one for the new plan under the same subscription. The moves accrued each period follow the plan held. A second recurring checkout beside a live subscription is refused with 409.
- Migration `0090_tiers.sql` widens `entitlement_grant_plan_known` to include `starter`. The `founding` move-ledger source and the `service_booking` table stay as history, and no new rows are written to either.
- Before deploying, create the Starter and Studio prices in Stripe and add them to the price map. Remove `founding_yearly` and `move_with_me` from the map: a key the build does not know stops the server at startup. Enable plan switching in the Stripe billing portal for the three products. Full list: `docs/notes/design/research/2026-09-27-subscription-tiers.md` §7.
