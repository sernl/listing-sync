# Teachouse 0.17.0

## Notify

- **Coloured toasts.** Every pop-up is coloured by what it says: green for a save, blue for information, amber when something was refused or needs a look, red for an error. They stack at the top right (across the top on a phone), at most three at once, and each goes after five seconds. Hovering or focusing one holds them; the close button, Escape and reduced motion all work as before.
- **Nothing missed.** A pop-up that went while you were looking elsewhere is kept in your notifications. One you closed or clicked is not.
- **The bell.** A bell sits between New resource and your account picture, the same size, with a count of what is unread. It opens the newest twenty with Mark read and Dismiss on each, Mark all read, and a link to the full page. On a phone it sits in the page header and opens as a sheet from the bottom.
- **Notifications page.** Each row carries its colour; unread rows are bold. Mark read and Delete per row, Mark all read and Delete read for the lot. The page no longer marks everything read just by opening it.
- **Finished runs** come in coloured by how they went: red if anything failed, amber if anything needs a look, green otherwise.

Deploy: migration `0100_notification_notice` runs on start (adds notice columns to `notification`, and widens its row policy to the user). New routes: `POST /v1/notifications`, `POST /v1/notifications/{id}/read`, `POST /v1/notifications/read-all`, `POST /v1/notifications/dismiss-read`, `DELETE /v1/notifications/{id}`; `GET /v1/notifications` now answers `unread` and a `source`/`tone` on each row.
