# Teachouse 0.18.0

The Terms of Service are live, teachouse.io tells search engines what it is and paints sooner, and the backoffice has a Payments page that lists every Stripe payment and issues refunds. Sale and theme days now follow New Zealand time, and the Pro plan's price keys are `pro_monthly` and `pro_yearly`.

## Terms

- **Terms of Service are live.** `/terms` is no longer a draft placeholder. It is the real agreement, last updated and effective 2 October 2026: twenty-one short numbered sections, each with a one-line "In short" summary, ending with "Questions people ask" (school accounts, cancelling, closing an account, passwords, files, refunds, using Teachouse outside New Zealand).
- **What the terms say.** Teachouse is an independent tool that acts in the teacher's own marketplace accounts from their own device, and is not affiliated with any marketplace. Accounts are 18+, one per person. The teacher keeps ownership and gives Teachouse a limited licence to run the service. Plans are in USD through Stripe, renew until cancelled and run to the end of the paid period; Move Packs are one-off and last 12 months; price changes get 30 days' notice. An unused Move Pack can be refunded within 14 days, as the pricing FAQ already said; other refunds are at our discretion except where New Zealand law requires one. New Zealand law governs; the Consumer Guarantees Act and Fair Trading Act are acknowledged for consumers, and business customers contract out of them. Liability is capped at what was paid in the last 12 months. The only contact is contact@teachouse.io, with no postal address.
- **Privacy Policy aligned.** The postal address is gone from the Contact section, "runs Teachouse from Auckland" now reads "in New Zealand", and the minimum age is 18 to match the terms (it said 13 with a parent's permission). Its date moves to 2 October 2026.

## SEO

teachouse.io now tells search engines and link previews what it is, and paints its first screen sooner.

- **No street address.** The footer no longer prints "Auckland 1072, New Zealand"; contact stays contact@teachouse.io in the footer links.
- **Search titles and descriptions.** The home page's title is "Teachouse – publish and sync your TPT and Tes listings" (54 characters), and its description ends on the availability sentence. Pricing, Terms and Privacy keep short "Page — Teachouse" titles. Every description is under 155 characters. `homeTitle` sits beside `availability` in `apps/landing/src/site.js`, and `just landing-copy-gate` lets both name the marketplaces.
- **Tags in every page's head.** A canonical URL, `robots` (index, follow), `theme-color` (the light `--ground` token, read from `site.css`), `lang="en-NZ"`, Open Graph (site name, `en_NZ` locale, the 1200×630 `images/og.png` card with its type, size and alt text) and Twitter `summary_large_image` cards.
- **Structured data.** One JSON-LD `@graph` per page, built in `apps/landing/src/seo.js`. Every page carries `Organization` (name, logo, email, no address). Pricing, Terms and Privacy add a `BreadcrumbList` (Teachouse › page). The home page and Pricing add `SoftwareApplication`, with one `Offer` per plan and per move pack in USD, read from `plans.generated.js`. The home page adds `FAQPage` with the FAQ section's questions.
- **`/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml`.** Both used to fall through to the console shell (HTML, 200), so Lighthouse counted 196 robots errors. The landing build now emits both. `robots.txt` allows the landing pages and disallows `/v1/`, `/api/`, `/ingest/`, `/downloads/`, `/maintenance/`, `/_app/` and every console route segment, and names the sitemap. The sitemap lists `/`, `/pricing/`, `/terms/` and `/privacy/`, each with a `lastmod` from `indexedPages` in `seo.js`. The Nix build has no git history, so those dates are written by hand: change a page's date in the same commit that changes the page. `served-artefacts` in `flake.nix` fails when a console route under `web/src/routes` has no `Disallow` line.
- **Headings and images.** On /pricing, the plan, comparison and pack headings are now `h2` under the page's `h1` (they look the same; `.rank-sub`), which fixes the heading order. Every `<img>` has `width`/`height`: the header and footer wordmarks, and the marketplace marks, whose sizes `src/intrinsic.js` reads from each file's own header at build time.
- **Lighter first paint.** `season.css` (35 KB) is linked under `media="not all"`: the browser fetches it at lowest priority without waiting on it, and the site-switch script turns it on only when a season or the banner is shown. Each page now waits on one 23 KB stylesheet, down from two (58 KB). The console screenshot ships as AVIF (62 KB desktop, 33 KB phone), with the WebP it came from as the fallback. The fonts already used `font-display: swap` and preloads.
- **Caching.** Landing pages are served `Cache-Control: public, max-age=300`, up from `no-cache`. `_astro/` assets keep `public, max-age=31536000, immutable`. Everything else keeps an hour plus its ETag. A browser that already holds a page sees a deploy or a maintenance switch within five minutes. Cloudflare already compresses (`content-encoding: br` and `gzip` on teachouse.io), so tam-server adds no compression.

## Payments

- **Admin → Payments.** A new page next to Pricing lists every money event from Stripe: payments, failed payments, refunds, disputes (shown as **Chargeback** with Stripe's status), paid and failed invoices, and subscriptions started or cancelled. The header shows this month's paid, refunded and disputed totals and counts (New Zealand calendar). Filter by kind, status, organisation and dates; open a row to see everything about the same charge or invoice. Each Stripe id has a copy button. On a phone each row is a card.
- **Refunds from the console.** **Refund** on a payment opens a side panel with the amount left on the charge filled in, Stripe's three reasons, a note for us, and **Email the customer**. The server checks Stripe's own running total before refunding, so a refund never goes past what is left ("You can refund at most $X on this payment."), a fully refunded charge cannot be refunded again ("This payment has already been refunded in full."), and pressing confirm twice refunds once. Every refund names who issued it, in the operators' trail.
- **Refund mail.** "We've refunded $X to your card. It can take 5–10 business days to show." goes to the organisation's people with a verified address when asked for, or later from **Send email**, which then reads **Sent on …**. The **Email customers when I refund** switch ticks the box by default and also mails customers about refunds made in Stripe's dashboard.
- **Sync from Stripe.** Reads the last 90 days of charges, refunds, disputes and invoices, so the page is complete for money that moved before this release. Safe to run any time; a sync never mails anybody.
- How to issue a refund, and what the customer sees: `docs/notes/runbooks/refunds.md`.

## Schedule timezone and price keys

- **Sale and theme days are New Zealand days.** A seasonal theme's From/Until and a sale's First/Last day now begin and end at midnight in `Pacific/Auckland` (`SITE_TIMEZONE` in `crates/tam-api/src/time.rs`), not at midnight UTC. A sale entered for 1 October opens at 11:00 UTC on 30 September while New Zealand keeps daylight time; its last day ends at midnight there, including on the 23- and 25-hour days when the clocks change. The admin Pricing and Site pages say "Days follow New Zealand time." once and no longer mention UTC; the Pricing form's "today" is New Zealand's.
- **`GET /v1/site` is `Cache-Control: private, max-age=60`** (was `public`). The browser still keeps it for a minute; Cloudflare and any other shared cache store nothing, so no edge TTL can hold a theme or maintenance switch longer than that minute.
- **Price keys `sync_monthly` / `sync_yearly` are now `pro_monthly` / `pro_yearly`** everywhere: `tam-limits`, the API, the generated console and landing tables, the NixOS module's `stripePriceMap` enum and the docs. Subscriptions store Stripe's price identifier, so none changes.
- **The Stripe price map must name all 11 keys.** `--stripe-price-map` already refused an unknown key; it now also refuses a map that leaves a key out, naming the missing keys, so the server does not start with a plan nobody can buy.
- **Look's "Moves this month" row is honest before a marketplace is connected.** `GET /v1/entitlement` carries `usage.free_moves_unlocked`, true once a storefront has been bound (which is when the five free moves are granted). Until then Billing reads Included "5 to try" and Left "5" with "Unlocked when you connect a marketplace" beneath, rather than "0 left, None left"; afterwards it shows the real figures.

### Deploy

1. Fleet config: rename the values in `services.teachouse.stripePriceMap` from `sync_monthly`/`sync_yearly` to `pro_monthly`/`pro_yearly`, and make sure all 11 keys are present — the evaluation fails on the old names and the server refuses an incomplete map.
2. Database, once, after the deploy (both idempotent):

   ```sql
   -- Discount windows written as UTC midnights become New Zealand midnights.
   UPDATE discount
      SET starts_at = (starts_at AT TIME ZONE 'UTC')::date::timestamp AT TIME ZONE 'Pacific/Auckland',
          ends_at   = (ends_at   AT TIME ZONE 'UTC')::date::timestamp AT TIME ZONE 'Pacific/Auckland'
    WHERE ended_at IS NULL
      AND (starts_at AT TIME ZONE 'UTC')::time = '00:00'
      AND (ends_at   AT TIME ZONE 'UTC')::time = '00:00';

   -- A one-off discount or code naming the old keys names the new ones.
   UPDATE discount
      SET price_keys = array_replace(array_replace(price_keys, 'sync_monthly', 'pro_monthly'),
                                     'sync_yearly', 'pro_yearly')
    WHERE price_keys && ARRAY['sync_monthly', 'sync_yearly']::text[];
   ```

3. Stripe (test and live): product "Teachouse Pro" (renamed from "Teachouse Sync" in 0.17.0, if not done yet); its monthly price's nickname and lookup key `pro_monthly`, its yearly price's `pro_yearly`. Price identifiers do not change.
