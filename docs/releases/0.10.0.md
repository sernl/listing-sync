# Teachouse 0.10.0 for Windows, Linux and Android

This beta adds explicit permission for marketplaces that have no official API, keeps your imported files on your own machines, and lets those machines share files directly with each other. Update the app before connecting or importing.

## Permission before work

- TES and TPT have no official API, so Teachouse works with them through your own sign-in on your own device. Before that starts, Teachouse now asks for your permission once per marketplace and records it under Account > Permissions.
- Connect opens the permission notice on this device or your phone. Nothing is connected, imported or published until you have ticked the confirmation and pressed I agree.
- Withdraw a permission under Account > Permissions at any time. Teachouse stops starting new work for that marketplace on every machine; logins already saved on your machines stay until you sign them out there.
- The notice states exactly what Teachouse keeps on its servers: catalogue details, listing copy and cover images. Your resource files are not part of it.

## Files stay on your machines

- Imported originals are kept on the machine that imported them, encrypted at rest, and listed under Files on this machine on the Marketplaces page. Keeping them can be switched off, and any file can be removed from that machine without touching your listing or the marketplace copy.
- A kept file can be viewed read-only inside the app, opened in another app, or used to cut a watermarked preview without downloading it again. You choose which pages the preview carries and which of those show your name.
- Two of your own machines can pass a kept file to each other directly, over an encrypted connection between the two of them. Teachouse's servers coordinate who holds what and never carry the file; a machine that cannot be reached is waited for and the app says so.

## Linux

- The desktop app is available for 64-bit Linux as an AppImage, with a .deb package on the same release.

This is a coordinated app and server update. Existing catalogue data, import history and saved marketplace logins are retained. On a phone that was reinstalled, saved logins may need to be entered again once.
