# Teachouse 0.20.0

## Copy

- **Refunds are stated plainly (terms section 9).** The clause "unless we agree or New Zealand law requires it" is gone. Move Packs: unused, within 14 days, full refund. Monthly plans: a month that has started is not refunded, and cancelling stops the next renewal. Yearly plans: a refund can be asked for at any time during the year. It covers the whole months not yet started, less one month's fee (yearly price × (unused whole months − 1) ÷ 12, never below zero), and the plan ends on the day of the refund. An Explain under the yearly rule works through an example on Pro's yearly price from the plan table: asked during month 4, 8 months are unused and the refund is 7 × $240 ÷ 12 = $140. The section's "In short" line, the terms' "Can I get a refund?" answer and the pricing FAQ say the same, and the FAQ links to `/terms/#refunds`. Section 15 (the Consumer Guarantees Act) is unchanged.
- **No more "young and still changing".** Section 11 now opens "we keep Teachouse running and improve it over time", and the line about features being "marked as early or coming soon" is gone. Nothing else on the landing described Teachouse as young, new, small or a startup.
- **Words no longer run together.** Astro drops a line break between text and a tag or an expression, so the terms read "contact@teachouse.iofrom", "Privacy Policysays", and the comparison table read "AI description fillComing soon". All three are fixed. The new `just landing-spacing-gate` (`apps/landing/scripts/check-spacing.mjs`) reads the built pages for a word touching an `a`, `strong`, `em`, `code` or `span` tag, and the `.astro` sources for text meeting a `{expression}` across a line break. It runs in `just landing-check`, after the copy gate.
- The pricing page's Explain style is now the shared `.explain` class (was `.move-explain`), which the terms use too.

Deploy: the terms changed in substance, so their version moves to `2026-10-04` (`TERMS_VERSION`, which the signup-consent change generates and which drives the terms page's "Last updated" and "Effective" dates). Section 18 promises 30 days' email notice before a change that matters; decide whether to send that notice or treat the refund change as in readers' favour.
