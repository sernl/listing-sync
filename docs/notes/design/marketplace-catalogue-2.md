# Marketplace catalogue, part two: widened list and logo sources

This extends `marketplace-catalogue.md` rather than repeating it.
Every claim below was read on 2026-09-05 and carries the URL it came from.
TES and TPT are absent by instruction: neither their sites nor their subdomains were contacted, so nothing here describes them and no logo is sourced for them.

## How to read the verification marks

`(fetched)` means the cited page or asset was retrieved directly on 2026-09-05, either through the page-reading tool or through a direct HTTP request that returned 200.
`(index)` means the cited page blocked automated retrieval and the quoted text reached me through a search engine's extract of that same page on 2026-09-05, so the wording is second-hand and should be re-read by a human before it is relied on in published copy.
`(unverified)` means only a third-party account exists and the primary page was not read.

Three findings worth stating up front.
The founder's TeachBuySell is real, Australian and live, but its listing data would not load for any automated client I used, so its inventory is unconfirmed.
The largest thing missing from part one is eduki, a Berlin company with a registered entity, a European multi-language footprint and a scale that puts it in the same tier as our live pair.
One candidate that reads well in search results, TipToe, appears to be invented by an AI-written listicle and has no primary existence at all.

## Group D — teaching-resource marketplaces new to this catalogue

### eduki

Legal name is Lehrerkolleg LK GmbH, registered at Rigaer Str. 29a, 10247 Berlin, in the commercial register of the Amtsgericht Charlottenburg under HRB 158506 B, with Max Maendler as managing director, per https://www.northdata.com/Lehrerkolleg%20LK%20GmbH,%20Berlin/Amtsgericht%20Charlottenburg%20(Berlin)%20HRB%20158506%20B and https://firmeneintrag.creditreform.de/10247/2012484100/LEHRERKOLLEG_LK_GMBH (index).
The company's own terms name the same entity as the operator of www.eduki.com, at https://eduki.com/agbs/German_2025-07-01_T&C.pdf (index).
Product name is eduki; it launched in 2016 as lehrermarktplatz.de and was renamed for international expansion (index).
Homepage is https://eduki.com/ and it is live (fetched).
An individual teacher can register as an author and sell uploaded teaching materials through https://eduki.com/de/material-teilen (fetched).
Prices are in euros, the footer offers roughly thirty language options, and the German-language site is labelled "eduki D-A-CH", so Germany, Austria and Switzerland are the core and Spanish, French, Italian and Greek are the stated expansion markets (fetched for the language list, index for the expansion claim).
Reach is claimed by third parties as over two million and, in one account, over three million teachers; neither figure was read on a first-party page, so neither should be printed (index).
The seller share is reported by a German teachers' forum as eduki taking 50% before a volume-based improvement, at https://www.lehrerforen.de/thread/64740-eduki-als-anbieter-nutzen/ (index); eduki's own help centre explains that the author licenses the material to eduki rather than selling to the buyer, at https://help.eduki.com/hc/de/articles/20178209332882-Muss-ich-ein-Gewerbe-anmelden (index).
No seller or partner API was found, and no brand or press page was found; press contact is a mailto address in the footer (fetched).

### TeachBuySell

Product name is TeachBuySell, one word, and the footer reads "© TeachBuySell, 2026" with the line "Made with ❤️ by Aussie teachers"; no legal entity and no ABN are published (fetched, https://teachbuysell.com.au/).
Homepage is https://teachbuysell.com.au/ and it returned 200 (fetched).
The seller path is open: the navigation carries "List your resource" and the footer carries "Become a seller", both pointing to https://teachbuysell.com.au/p/sell, which also links seller FAQs (fetched).
The site prices in AUD, states "Pay in AUD — no currency conversion hassles for Australian educators", is tagged "Australian Primary & ECE resources", and carries an Acknowledgement of Country (fetched).
No commission or seller fee is published on the sell page, and no API is mentioned anywhere (fetched).
It runs on Sharetribe, evidenced by its `sharetribe-assets.imgix.net` asset host (fetched).
The qualification that matters: on both retrievals the page rendered the banner "Oh no, something went wrong. Please check your network connection and try again." and no listings appeared, so the marketplace shell is live but its inventory is unconfirmed by me and a human should open it in a browser before it is tiled.
Third-party corroboration that it is a real project exists in a build case study at https://www.journeyh.io/portfolio/teachbuysell and a Facebook page at https://www.facebook.com/teachbuysell/ (index).

### Lesson Planned

### TeachersTrading.com

Product name is TeachersTrading.com, tagline "Your Home For Educational Materials"; no legal entity is published and the footer reads "© Copyright 2026 TeachersTrading.com. All Rights Reserved." (fetched, https://teacherstrading.com/).
Homepage is https://teacherstrading.com/ and it is live, with a cart, recent listings and individual seller shops such as "Ms. Cláudia's Creative Corner" (fetched).
It invites users to "Join our community of educators to buy and sell teaching materials" and carries a "Become A Seller" section (fetched).
Two tiers are published: Basic Membership at "60%-80% Commission Levels" and Premium Membership at "80% Commission Level"; the page does not say explicitly that these are the seller's share rather than the platform's, so a human should confirm before the figure is repeated (fetched).
No country is stated, but the category list is heavy with Hebrew, Israeli Culture, Jewish Culture and Judaic Studies, which suggests a United States and Israel audience rather than a general one (fetched).
No seller or partner API was found, and no brand page was found.
Recommendation: this is the weakest of the new teaching marketplaces on breadth of audience, and it is a candidate for the reserve list rather than the grid.

### Kahoot! Marketplace

Product name is Kahoot! Marketplace, at https://kahoot.com/marketplace/, with the creator path at https://kahoot.com/marketplace-creator/ (fetched for the homepage assets, index for the programme description).
It is live in 2026: the support section at https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/sections/5261543619987-Marketplace-Verified-creators was last updated 30 July 2026 and a company blog post about creator content is dated February 2026 (index).
Verified creators and publishers sell learning content, and a creator earns money for each course sold (index, https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/5261720619795-What-is-Kahoot-marketplace).
Two gates apply: a creator must pass an editorial review to become a Verified creator, needing a profile and at least three public kahoots, and must hold a paid individual plan to sell (index, https://kahoot.com/community/verified-creator/).
Content is Kahoot-native courses and kahoots authored in Kahoot's own tools rather than uploaded files, which makes it structurally like Boom Learning and unlike a file marketplace (index).
Recommendation: hold in reserve rather than tile, because the paid-plan-plus-approval gate means a seller cannot simply act on it, and because our unit of sync is a file.

## Group E — digital-product platforms not previously covered

### Creative Fabrica

Legal name is Creative Fabrica B.V., headquartered in Amsterdam, founded 2016 by Anca Stefan and Roemie Hillenaar, per https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/creative-fabrica and the terms referenced from https://www.creativefabrica.com/license/ (index).
Homepage is https://www.creativefabrica.com/ and it returned 403 to two independent automated clients on 2026-09-05, so live status rests on the search index (index).
An individual designer can open a store free, each listing is vetted before going live, and the stated split is 75% on sales the designer refers and 50% on sales Creative Fabrica refers, per https://www.creativefabrica.com/open-store/ and https://craftindustryalliance.org/considering-selling-on-creative-fabrica-heres-what-you-need-to-know/ (index).
Its relevance to teachers is real but adjacent: it sells clip art, fonts and cut files rather than classroom resources, and a storefront named Teacher's Clipart exists at https://www.creativefabrica.com/designer/teachers-clipart/ (index).
No public seller API and no developer documentation were found; uploads go through a web dashboard with a manual review step (index).
Recommendation: do not tile as a marketplace we would sync to, because there is no write path, and revisit only if a founder wants a clip-art lane.

### Teachable

Homepage is https://www.teachable.com/ and it is live (fetched).
It sells digital downloads natively, not only courses, per its own product page at https://www.teachable.com/digital-downloads (index).
It is a storefront rather than a marketplace, so it is a distribution surface for a seller's own audience and not a discovery venue.
A 2026 platform survey states that Teachable relies primarily on Zapier rather than direct API integration, at https://lms-guide.com/guides/lms-apis-developer-tools-complete-guide/ (index).
Recommendation: exclude, on the same reasoning as Podia and Ko-fi in part one — with no documented product-write API, a tile would promise something we cannot build.

### Thinkific

Homepage is https://www.thinkific.com/ and it returned 403 to automated retrieval on 2026-09-05, so live status rests on the search index (index).
It does publish a real API: the Admin API is documented at https://developers.thinkific.com/api/api-documentation with base URL https://api.thinkific.com/api/public/v1, OAuth or subdomain-plus-API-key authentication, and JSON responses (index).
Two constraints undercut it.
API access is gated behind the Pro plan plus Growth package or higher, so an ordinary teacher-seller has no key (index).
And its product model is course-first; a competitor's comparison claims digital downloads require a workaround, at https://www.heightsplatform.com/blog/can-you-sell-digital-download-products-on-thinkific (index) — that source sells a rival product, so weigh it low and confirm before repeating.
Recommendation: exclude, as a course platform whose API is paywalled above our sellers' tier.

### Amazon KDP

Homepage is https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/ and the programme is live (index).
An individual can publish printable workbooks and planners as low-content paperbacks, which must be marked as low content at upload and receive no free ISBN; Amazon's content policy is at https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390 (index).
Amazon tightened enforcement against duplicate, mass-generated and minimally differentiated low-content titles, per https://pubnook.com/article/amazon-kdp-low-content-book-policy-2026-whats-allowed-and-what-gets-removed (index).
No public KDP publishing API was found; the Selling Partner API is a separate programme covering Amazon marketplace selling, not KDP publishing (index).
Recommendation: exclude, because the artefact is a printed book rather than a digital download, and because there is no API path.
