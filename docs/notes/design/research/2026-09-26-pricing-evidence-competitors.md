# Pricing Evidence: Competitor Price Sheets

Companion to [2026-09-20-pricing-evidence-market.md](2026-09-20-pricing-evidence-market.md). That pack's §1 gave category ranges and §3 covered teacher tool spend. This pack re-fetches every vendor on **2026-09-26** and records the shape of each offer: metered unit, free tier, trial and card policy, annual discount, and guarantee. Where this pack disagrees with the 09-20 pack, this one is newer and first-hand.

**Conventions.** Unless a row is marked otherwise, every figure was fetched first-hand from the vendor's own page on 2026-09-26.
- `[SECONDARY]`: third-party page.
- `[NOT VERIFIED]`: the vendor page could not be read; the reason is given.
- `[INFERENCE]`: my own reading of a page, not a vendor statement.

Annual discount % is computed as 1 − (annual monthly-equivalent ÷ monthly price). GBP prices exclude VAT unless stated.

**Norm.** On both marketplaces Teachouse bridges (TPT, Tes), **publishing a listing costs $0**; TPT's only gate is the one-time or annual seller membership. The closest precedents a teacher-seller will know are Etsy's $0.20 and the cross-lister band of **$0.13–$0.18 per new listing**.
