# Labels and collections

Labels are your own words for sorting resources, like a term, a bundle or a sale. Only you see them. A collection is a group of resources, in an order you choose, that you can publish, fill in or export in one go.

## Add a label

1. Open **Catalogue → Labels**.
2. Type the word under **New label**, for example "Summer term". A label can be up to 60 characters.
3. Under **Put it on**, tick the resources that should carry it.
4. Press **Create label on** and the number of resources.

A resource can carry up to twenty labels. A resource's own page lists its labels and collections under **Filed under**.

## Use your labels

- The number on each label under **Your labels** is how many resources carry it. Click a label to see those resources.
- Labels also narrow the **Resources** list and [Analytics](/guides/analytics).
- A [schedule](/guides/schedules) can send "everything labelled Autumn term". It looks up the label each time it runs, so a resource you label later goes out on the next run.

To rename or delete a label, press **⋮** beside it and choose **Rename** or **Delete**. Deleting a label takes it off every resource; the resources stay.

## Labels added by an import

An import adds its own labels, such as TPT or Tes, so you can find what it brought in. They show a lock. You cannot rename or delete them, but you can add your own labels next to them.

## Make a collection

1. Open **Catalogue → Collections** and press **New collection**.
2. Give it a **Name**, and a **Note to yourself** if you like.
3. Press **Create**.

Look has 1 label, 1 template and 1 collection, Starter 5 of each, Pro 10 and Studio no limit. See [Plans](/guides/plans).

## Put resources in order

A collection page works in two steps, shown across the top: **Resources** and **Publish**.

1. Under **Resources in it**, press **Add resources**.
2. Tick the resources, or search for them, and press **Add** with the number you ticked.
3. Use **Up** and **Down** to put them in the order you want.

**Remove** takes a resource out of the collection only. It stays in your catalogue.

## The order matters

Everything you do to a collection follows its order. Publishing sends resources in that order, a template fills them in that order, and the spreadsheet lists them in that order.

## Publish a collection

1. In **Publish**, choose the marketplace.
2. Choose **As drafts** (you publish them on the marketplace yourself) or **Live** (buyers see them straight away).
3. Press **Preview**. The table says what would happen to each resource and why.
4. Press **Publish** and the number of resources that will go.

A resource already on that marketplace is left alone rather than published twice. A resource that is **Blocked** says what is missing; fix it on the resource, then press **Preview again**. Previewing never publishes anything. We can't undo a live publish on Tes.

## Also for this collection

Under **Also for this collection**:

- **Apply a template…** fills in one template's fields on every resource in the collection. See [Templates](/guides/templates).
- **Add labels** puts one or more labels on every resource in it. Separate labels with commas.
- **Export CSV** makes a spreadsheet of just this collection: one row per resource, with each marketplace's status, price and link.

These always apply to the whole collection. To leave a resource out, take it out of the collection first.

## Which to use

Use a label to find things, and to tell a schedule what to send. Use a collection when you want to do the same thing to several resources in a set order.
