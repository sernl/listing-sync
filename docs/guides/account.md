# Signing in and your account

Everything about your account is under **Account → Account settings**: its name, your profile, emails, passkeys and the browsers and devices signed in to it. Your plan and payments are under **Account → Billing**; see [Your plan and what it allows](/guides/plans).

## The tour

The first time you sign in, a short tour shows you around the console: **Import**, **Catalogue**, **New resource**, **Automate**, **Marketplaces** and **Billing**. Its last card is **Start here: download the app**: "Your marketplaces are connected in the Teachouse app, so get it on your computer first." Press **Get the app** to go to step 1 on **Marketplaces**. See [Why you need the Teachouse app](/guides/why-the-app).

To see the tour again, open **Account → Help and guides** and press **Show me around**.

## One Teachouse account per shop

Each marketplace shop can belong to only one Teachouse account. If a second account tries to connect the same shop, Teachouse will not allow it.

1. Decide which account the shop belongs to.
2. Disconnect the shop from any other account first.
3. Connect it to the account you want. See [Connecting a marketplace](/guides/connecting).

Every account gets its free moves once. Moving a shop to a new account does not give it a second set.

## Your account name

1. Open **Account → Account settings**.
2. Under **Account name**, type the name you want in **Display name**.
3. Press **Save display name**.

Buyers never see this name or your email address. Buyers only see what you publish.

## Where emails go

Under **Notifications**, turn on **Email me when an import or move finishes** to get an email when one is done. Emails go to the address you sign in with, shown under the switch.

## Passkeys

A passkey signs you in with the fingerprint, face or PIN that unlocks your device.

1. Open **Account → Account settings** and find **Passkeys**.
2. If you like, give it a name under **Name for the new passkey**, for example "Alice’s laptop".
3. Press **Add a passkey**.
4. Confirm on your device.

If your browser does not support passkeys, the panel says so. Sign in with your password, and add a passkey later from a browser that does. Press **Remove** beside a passkey to stop using it.

## Browsers and devices

**Browser sign-ins** lists every browser signed in to your account. Press **End sign-in** on one you no longer use.

**Device sign-ins** lists every device running the Teachouse app, such as Alice’s laptop and Alice’s phone. For each device you see when it last checked in, which marketplace logins are saved on it, and a link to **View files on this device**.

To sign a device out:

1. Open **Account → Account settings** and find **Device sign-ins**.
2. Press **Sign out** beside the device.
3. Confirm.

A signed-out device stops working for your account. It removes your marketplace logins the next time it goes online; until then, the logins stay on that device. Nothing already saved in Teachouse is deleted. To use the device again, open the app on it and choose **Sign this device back in**.

## Forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, press **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your email and press **Send the link**.
3. Open the link in the email.
4. Type your new password twice and press **Change password**.
