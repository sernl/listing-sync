# Who holds the copyright

Tell us who owns the copyright for the work you sell. This is your own statement, and it goes with everything you publish. You say it once for each shop.

## Add it

1. Open **Marketplaces**.
2. Go to step 3, **Who made this work**.
3. Press **Declare** beside the shop.
4. Under **Your name, as the copyright holder**, type the person or business that owns the copyright, for example "Alice and John" if you made the work together.
5. Press **Save**.

## Add it before you publish

TPT refuses anything you publish until you declare it. Tes does not need it, but you can declare it there too.

Until you declare it for TPT, **Automations** shows **Copyright needed** on **Migrations**. Add it before you first publish and TPT never turns a listing away for this.

## Adding it later does not resend

Your statement only applies to what you send after saving it. Listings TPT already refused are not sent again for you.

1. Declare the copyright holder.
2. Publish those resources to TPT again.

## Changing it

Change it whenever the answer changes, for example when you start selling under a business name.

1. Open **Marketplaces** and go to step 3, **Who made this work**.
2. Press **Change** beside the shop.
3. Type the new name and press **Save**.

Teachouse uses the new statement from your next send.

## Work you did not make

Name the copyright owner honestly. If you sell work licensed from someone else, choose the terms you sell under when you create the resource. See [Creating a resource](/guides/new-resource).
