# Connecting a marketplace

Connect a marketplace once. Teachouse can then read your shop and send listings to it.

Open **Marketplaces**. The page walks you through three steps: **Get the app**, **Connect your shops** and **Who made this work**. A tick shows beside each step you have finished.

## Step 1: Get the app

TPT and Tes have no official way for other services to connect, so you connect them in the free Teachouse app. The app signs in for you on your own device, and Teachouse never holds your passwords. Read [Why you need the Teachouse app](/guides/why-the-app) for more.

1. Open **Marketplaces**.
2. In step 1, **Get the app**, press the download button for your device: **Download for Windows**, **Download for Linux** or **Download for Android™**.
3. Install the app and sign in with your Teachouse account.

There is no iPhone or Mac app yet. Everything else in Teachouse works in the app or in any web browser.

The first time you open the app on a device, a short tour shows you around. Its last card, **Then work from anywhere**, has a **Connect a marketplace** button that takes you straight to step 2.

## Step 2: Connect your shops

Do this in the app, not in your web browser.

1. Open **Marketplaces** in the Teachouse app.
2. Press **Connect** on the shop's card.
3. The first time, Teachouse asks your permission. Read what it will do, tick the box and press **I agree**.
4. Sign in to that marketplace in the window the app opens.
5. Check the card now says **Signed in**.

In a web browser, **Connect** takes you back to step 1 with the note "Open the Teachouse app on your device to connect."

Each card says which device you are signed in on, for example "Signed in on Alice’s laptop. Your login stays on that device."

On Windows, connecting TPT or Tes could fail if signing in took a long time. The current app fixes this. If connecting failed for you before, update the app and press **Connect** again.

## Step 3: Who made this work

Say who holds the copyright, once per shop. TPT refuses anything you publish until you do. See [Who holds the copyright](/guides/copyright).

## Keep the app open for marketplace work

Imports, publishing and schedules on TPT and Tes run in the app. They wait for a device where the app is open and signed in, and start again when you open it.

## One Teachouse account per shop

Each shop belongs to one Teachouse account. If your shop is already connected to another account, disconnect it there first. See [Signing in and your account](/guides/account).

## Disconnect a shop

1. Open **Marketplaces**.
2. On the shop's card, press **Disconnect TPT from your account** (or **Disconnect TES from your account**).

Once you disconnect, Teachouse starts nothing new on that marketplace. Listings that are already live stay live.

To remove the login from one device only, open **Marketplaces** in the app on that device and press **Sign out of TPT on this device** (or TES).

## Check it is working

Open **Account → Marketplace status** to see whether each marketplace is working right now. You do not need to sign in to see that page, so you can send us the link when something looks wrong.
