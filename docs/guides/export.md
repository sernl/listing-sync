# Exporting your catalogue

An export is a spreadsheet of your resources. It opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

## Export your catalogue

**Export** walks you through three steps: **What**, **Format** and **Download**.

1. Open **Catalogue → Export**.
2. Under **What**, choose **Every resource**, or **One collection** and pick the collection.
3. Under **Format**, **CSV spreadsheet** is already chosen.
4. Under **Download**, press **Download CSV**.

## Export one collection

You can also export from the collection itself.

1. Open **Catalogue → Collections** and open the collection.
2. Press **Export CSV**.

Resources are listed in the same order as the collection. See [Labels and collections](/guides/labels-and-collections).

## What is in the file

Each resource gets one row: its title, price, currency, labels, and when it was created and last changed. For each marketplace (TES, TPT and Etsy) the row shows the listing's status, the price you set there and a link to the live page. **What’s in it?** on the **Download** step says the same.

The file holds details only. It does not include your resource files, previews or marketplace logins. Your files stay on your devices. See [Where your files live](/guides/your-files).

## The file name

Each file is named `teachouse-resources-<date>.csv`, dated in UTC, so your exports sort in the order you made them.

## Made when you ask

Teachouse makes the file when you press **Download CSV** and does not keep it. After you change something, export again to get an up-to-date file.
