# Your plan and what it allows

One thing is counted: a **move**. A move is one resource committed to the other marketplace. It is counted when you commit it, after duplicates have been merged, whether it lands as a draft or as a live listing. Reading, editing and importing cost nothing.

## Look — free

- Import both marketplaces and read everything you have.
- Keep up to 500 resources.
- Connect as many marketplaces as you like.
- Five free moves for each shop, once ever.
- Publishing spends moves.

## Sync — $29 a month, or $240 a year

- 25 moves a month, and unused moves stack up to 75.
- Schedules, pulls and figures.
- 200 AI fills a month, once AI arrives.

## The 90-day edit window

After you move a listing, edit it and send the change for 90 days without spending another move.

## Founding 100

Annual only: $180 for the first year, $192 in years two and three, list price from year four, plus 20 moves. It closes at 100 members or on 31 December 2026, whichever comes first.

## Move with me — $99

Book a session and we do your move with you. It is a booking and brings no moves; buy a pack for those.

## Buy, change or cancel

1. Open **Account → Subscription**.
2. Choose a plan or a pack.
3. Manage or cancel the subscription from the same page.
