# Your plan and what it allows

Teachouse counts one thing: **moves**.

A move is publishing one imported resource onto one marketplace. Publishing a resource to Tes and TPT is 2 moves; to Tes alone is 1 move.

- A move counts when you publish, whether the listing arrives as a draft or goes live.
- Duplicates are merged first, so the same resource never counts twice.
- Reading, editing and importing are free.

## Look (trial) — free

- Import from both marketplaces and see everything you have.
- Keep up to 500 resources.
- Connect as many marketplaces as you like.
- Get five free moves, once only.
- Publishing uses moves.

## Starter (subscription) — $12 a month, or $96 a year

- Edit resources in Teachouse and sync the edits to all your shops once a day.
- Set up schedules.
- Get 10 moves a month. Unused moves build up to 30.
- 5 templates and 5 collections.

## Sync (subscription) — $29 a month, or $240 a year

- Everything in Starter, with edits sent every few hours.
- See statistics for every shop.
- Publish automatically with rules.
- Get 25 moves a month. Unused moves build up to 75.
- 20 templates and 20 collections.

## Studio (subscription) — $59 a month, or $480 a year

- Everything in Sync, with edits sent every hour.
- Get 100 moves a month. Unused moves build up to 300.
- Unlimited templates and collections.
- Priority support: we answer within a day.

Every plan can add a watermarked preview of your file. AI description fill is coming soon on the paid plans.

Pack moves last 12 months from the day you buy them.

## The 90-day edit window

After you move a listing, you have 90 days to edit it once and send the change. That edit does not use another move.

## Buy, change or cancel

1. Open **Account → Billing**.
2. Choose a plan or a pack. The switch above the plans shows monthly or yearly prices.
3. Already paying? Change to another plan with **Manage billing**.
4. To stop renewing, press **Cancel plan**. Your plan keeps working until the date on the page, and **Keep my plan** undoes it until then.
5. **Update** changes the card you pay with; **Invoices** lists every bill, each with a link to view or download it.
