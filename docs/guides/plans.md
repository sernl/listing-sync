# Your plan and what it allows

Teachouse counts one thing: **moves**.

A move is publishing one imported resource onto one marketplace. Publishing a resource to Tes and TPT is 2 moves; to Tes alone is 1 move.

- A move counts when you publish, whether the listing arrives as a draft or goes live.
- Duplicates are merged first, so the same resource never counts twice.
- Reading, editing and importing are free.

## Look (Trial) — free

- Import from both marketplaces and see everything you have.
- Keep up to 500 resources.
- Connect as many marketplaces as you like.
- Get five free moves for each shop, once only.
- Publishing uses moves.

## Sync (Subscription) — $29 a month, or $240 a year

- Edit resources in Teachouse and sync the edits to all your shops.
- See statistics for every shop.
- Set up schedules.
- Get 25 moves a month. Unused moves build up to 75.
- Get 200 AI fills a month, once AI arrives.

## The 90-day edit window

After you move a listing, you have 90 days to edit it once and send the change. That edit does not use another move.

## Founding 100

Yearly only. You pay $180 for the first year and $192 in years two and three. From year four you pay the list price. You also get 20 moves. The offer closes at 100 members or on 31 December 2026, whichever comes first.

## Move with me — $99

Book a session and we move your shop with you. The booking includes no moves, so buy a pack for those.

## Buy, change or cancel

1. Open **Account → Plan and moves**.
2. Choose a plan or a pack.
3. Manage or cancel your subscription on the same page.
