# Creating a resource

A resource is one thing you sell. You write it once, and every marketplace you choose gets the same words.

## The four steps

Open **Catalogue → New resource**. The form is one page with four steps, shown across the top: **Files**, **Details**, **Where it is listed** and **Create**. A step gets a tick when it has what it needs. Click a step's name to jump to it.

Nothing is sent to a marketplace while you fill in the form.

## Start from a template

If you have saved a template, the form opens with **Optional: start from a template** above the steps.

1. Choose your template under **Template**.
2. Read the line under it. It says which fields the template filled in.
3. Press **Undo** if you want the form back as it was.

A template only fills empty fields, so nothing you have already typed is replaced. See [Templates](/guides/templates).

## Step 1: Files

1. Drag your file onto **Drag and drop your files here, or browse**, or click it to choose one. You can add more than one.
2. Tick **Keep this ZIP as one file** if you are adding a ZIP that buyers should get as it is.

Files you add here are uploaded to Teachouse and kept there, so they are marked **Uploaded**. They count towards your plan's storage, and the line under the list shows how much you have used. Files you [import](/guides/importing) work differently: they stay on your own devices. See [Where your files live](/guides/your-files).

Your first file makes the thumbnail. TPT shows up to four thumbnails, and the first one is the cover.

## Make a preview

The preview is a free sample buyers see before they buy. You make it from pages of your PDF.

1. Press **Make a preview from your file**.
2. Tick the pages buyers can see, or press **Select all**.
3. Leave **Watermark with my name** ticked to print your shop name faintly across each page. You can change the name in the box.
4. Tick **Preview the result** to see a page as buyers will see it.
5. Press **Make preview**.

The preview is made in your browser, then uploaded as the resource's preview.

## Step 2: Details

Give the resource a **Title**, a **Description** and a price. The counter under each box shows how many characters you have left.

### The description editor

Choose how you write the description:

- **Rich text** looks the way it will on TPT. Use the **B**, **I**, **•**, **1.** and **Link** buttons for bold, italics, lists and links.
- **Markdown** is plain text with marks in it, such as `**bold**`. The **B**, **I**, **•** and **1.** buttons add the marks for you.

Switching between the two converts what you have written. Tes always gets plain text: paragraphs, and lists with a dash or a number on each line. Press **Which?** in the form for a reminder.

### Grades

Under **Categories**, pick grades in either **American (Grades)** or **British (Years)**. Teachouse translates them for the other marketplaces. Switching the toggle only changes the names you see; the grades you picked stay picked.

<!-- shot: /resources/new, the Grade Level picker with British (Years) chosen -->
![Picking grades in British years](/v1/guides/images/43ac79377bb93389c205ee7c5c265e15c39b595cae2f45f94624726631e729a6)

Each marketplace has its own subjects, grades, licences and resource types. Fill in your own words once. [Target term rules](/guides/target-terms) turn them into each marketplace's words.

## Step 3: Where it is listed

1. Tick the marketplaces this resource goes to.
2. Answer the questions each marketplace adds below the tiles. TPT, for example, asks you to choose one of its two copyright statements and a **Tax Code**.

Open **What each marketplace shows** to see, marketplace by marketplace, what buyers there will read.

## Step 4: Create

Choose how the resource starts under **Product Status**:

- **Draft, visible only to you** keeps it to yourself.
- **Make listing active on my selected marketplaces** means buyers can find it once it is published.

If anything is still missing, it is listed under **Click an error to go to it.** Click one to jump to the field. When the list is gone, press **Create resource** in the bar at the bottom of the page.

## Publish it

Creating a resource does not send it anywhere. To publish:

1. Open the resource from **Catalogue → Resources**.
2. Press **Publish…** at the bottom of the page.
3. Choose **Leave as a draft** or **Publish live**.
4. Tick the marketplaces. Each one says whether it is **ready to send** or what it still needs.
5. Press **Send to 1**. The number is how many marketplaces you ticked.

You cannot undo a live publish to Tes from Teachouse, so check the resource first. Set a [target price](/guides/target-prices) before you publish, or the resource will be held back.
