# Creating a resource

A resource is one thing you sell. You write it once, and every marketplace uses the same words.

## Fill in the form

1. Open **Catalogue → New resource**.
2. Work down the sections in order: details, description, categories, standards, price, status.
3. Save as a draft at any time.

## Start from a template

Choose a template in the first section and your usual answers are filled in for you. You can change anything afterwards. See [Templates](/guides/templates).

## What each marketplace asks for

Each marketplace has its own subjects, grades, licences and resource types. Fill in your own words once. [Target term rules](/guides/target-terms) turn them into each marketplace's words.

## Grades

You can show grades in British or American terms. Use the toggle to switch. The grades you picked stay the same.

## Preview and watermark

1. Upload your PDF.
2. Tick the pages buyers can see.
3. Turn on the watermark to print your name faintly across each preview page.

The preview is made on your own computer and then uploaded as the resource's preview.

## Draft or active

- **Draft** keeps the resource in your catalogue only.
- **Active** means it can be published, scheduled or moved.

Set a target price before you publish, or the resource will be held back.
