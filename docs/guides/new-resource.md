# Creating a resource

A resource is one thing you sell, with one set of words that every marketplace draws from.

## Fill the form

1. Open **Catalogue → New resource**.
2. Work down the bands in order: details, description, categories, standards, price, status.
3. Save as a draft at any point.

## Start from a template

Choose a template in the first band and your usual answers arrive filled in. Change anything you like afterwards. See [Templates](/guides/templates).

## What each marketplace asks for

Each marketplace asks for its own subjects, grades, licence and resource type. Fill your own words once; [target term rules](/guides/target-terms) turn them into each marketplace's vocabulary.

## Grades

Grades read in British or American words from the same set. Switch the labels with the toggle; the selection underneath does not change.

## Preview and watermark

1. Upload your PDF.
2. Tick the pages buyers may see.
3. Turn on the watermark to draw your name faintly across each previewed page.

The preview is built on your own machine and uploaded as the resource's preview.

## Draft or active

- **Draft** keeps it in your catalogue only.
- **Active** makes it eligible for a publish, a schedule or a migration.

Set a target price before you publish, or the row will be held back.
