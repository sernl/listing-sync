# Why you need the Teachouse app

Most of Teachouse works in your web browser. One job needs the free Teachouse app on your own computer or phone: connecting marketplaces that have no official way for other services to connect. Today that is TPT and Tes.

## What the app does for you

- **It signs in for you.** You sign in to TPT and Tes inside the app, on your own device. Your password goes to the marketplace, never to Teachouse, and your sign-in stays on that device.
- **It keeps your files with you.** Files you import stay on your device. When you open one in your browser, Teachouse passes it across from your device without keeping a copy.
- **It does the marketplace work.** Reading your shop during an import, sending a listing when you publish, and running your schedules all happen in the app.

## What works in your browser

Everything else works in the app or in any browser where you sign in: importing, editing resources, labels and collections, templates, publishing, schedules, pricing, analytics and billing.

## Get the app

1. Open **Marketplaces**.
2. In step 1, **Get the app**, download the app for your device.
3. Install it and sign in with your Teachouse account.
4. In the app, open **Marketplaces** and press **Connect** on each shop. See [Connecting a marketplace](/guides/connecting).

There is an app for Windows, Linux and Android. There is no iPhone or Mac app yet.

## Keep the app open for marketplace work

Imports, publishing and schedules on TPT and Tes wait for a device with the app open and signed in. If every device is closed, the work waits and starts again when you open the app.

## Files you upload yourself

Files you upload in Teachouse, for example a file for a new resource, are stored by Teachouse so you can use them anywhere, until you delete them. See [Where your files live](/guides/your-files).
