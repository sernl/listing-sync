# Moving a shop to another marketplace

A migration takes resources you already sell on one marketplace and lists them on another. You set it up in four steps: **Where**, **What**, **Preview** and **Start**.

## Before you start

1. Connect both marketplaces on **Marketplaces**. TPT and Tes are connected in the free Teachouse app, so get the app on a device first (step **1 Get the app**). See [Why you need the Teachouse app](/guides/why-the-app).
2. Import the shop you are moving from.
3. Say who made the work in step **3 Who made this work**. You do this once for each marketplace you send to. See [Who holds the copyright](/guides/copyright).

## Step 1: Where

1. Open **Automations → Migrations**.
2. Under **From**, choose the marketplace you sell on now.
3. Under **To**, choose the marketplace you want to add.
4. Choose **Copy** or **Move**.

<!-- shot: /automations/migration, step 1 Where set to TPT → TES with Copy chosen -->
![Step 1 of a migration: from TPT to Tes, as a copy](/v1/guides/images/8b1a07f3ce72a6ea9048b8f97efc998f042bca0a79ff603d4d11ad84b01de112)

- **Copy** keeps the original listing where it is.
- **Move** adds the listing to the new marketplace, then removes the original.

If you are not sure, pick **Copy**. You can take the original down later.

## Step 2: What

Choose **Every resource on** the marketplace you picked, or **Let me choose** and tick the ones you want.

## Step 3: Preview

Press **Preview**. The table shows what would happen to each resource:

- **Will create**: it will be listed on the new marketplace.
- **Already there**: it is already listed there, so it is left alone. **Open on** takes you to the listing.
- **Blocked**: something is missing. The **Why** column says what.

A preview sends nothing and costs nothing. If you change anything above it, press **Preview again**.

![A migration preview: three to add, two already there, one blocked](/v1/guides/images/91337f50e6a420dde04da89d59339f4bf0b3b97d9dbd0b28432cd69327fcc4bc)

## What it costs

Each resource you list on the other marketplace is one move. Under the preview you see how many moves you have and how many this migration uses. Resources that are already there or blocked use none. See [Your plan and what it allows](/guides/plans) for how you get moves.

## Step 4: Start

When the preview looks right, press the button in **Start**, for example **Copy 3 resources**. The new listings are made as drafts, so you can check them on the marketplace before they go live.

<!-- shot: /automations/migration, step 4 Start ready to copy 3 resources as drafts -->
![Ready to start: copy three resources, as drafts](/v1/guides/images/9599c8fd2069c0f34ecf03ff41d9c2174c493aed3b59537eabe6a6a89e9676fc)

If **Start** says **Declare the copyright holder first**, press **Declare for TPT** (or **Declare for TES**) and finish step **3 Who made this work** on **Marketplaces**. Then come back and start.

## When it runs

TPT and Tes have no official way in, so a device does the work: your computer or phone with the Teachouse app open and signed in. The migration waits until one of your devices checks in, then that device lists each resource. Your files stay on your own devices. See [Where your files live](/guides/your-files).

Keep the app open until the migration is finished.

## What holds a resource back

The **Why** column tells you what to fix. Common reasons:

- **that marketplace requires Licence**: no licence is set for that marketplace yet. Set one in [Mapping your words to a marketplace's](/guides/target-terms).
- A missing price. Set one in [Setting target prices](/guides/target-prices).
- **already on its way to Tes, and will show there once your device sends it**: a schedule or an earlier migration is already sending it.
- You do not have enough moves.

Under the table, **Set prices** and **Set terms** take you straight to the page that fixes it. Then preview again. Resources already listed are never listed twice.

## Follow a migration

Every copy and move you start appears under **History** on the same page. **Show** lets you see only copies or only moves, and **Activity** lists what happened in order.

<!-- shot: /automations/migration, History with one TPT → TES copy -->
![Your migrations in History](/v1/guides/images/db334dd12bb5493755cb023c2855d10604d29a10e95b5bc3093f5a91ecd425d3)

To see each listing being sent, open **Automations → Updates** and look under **Runs**. See [Keeping marketplaces up to date](/guides/updates).
