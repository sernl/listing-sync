# Mapping your words to a marketplace's

Each marketplace has its own list of subjects, grades, licences and resource types. A target term rule says which of their words your resources land under.

## Write a rule

1. Open **Automations → Target terms**.
2. Choose the marketplace.
3. Pick the word of yours to match.
4. Choose the marketplace's term it becomes and save.

## What you must map

- **Licence.** Each marketplace names its own licences; pick the one you sell under.
- **Resource type.** A marketplace will refuse a listing filed under nothing.
- **Subjects and grades.** Map the ones you use most; the rest can wait.

## Keyword matching

A rule can match on a keyword found in a title or description. Matching reads the words only: formatting is stripped before the match, so bold text matches the same as plain.

## Unmatched words

A word no rule covers becomes an unmatched word.

1. Open **Unmatched words**.
2. Read the word and where it came from.
3. Choose the marketplace's term for it.

Each one you answer stays answered, and the answer is used the next time a run meets that word.

## Templates fill, rules map

A template fills fields on a new resource. A target term rule translates a filled field into the marketplace's own vocabulary. See [Templates](/guides/templates).
