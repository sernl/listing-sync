# Mapping your words to a marketplace's

Each marketplace has its own list of licences and resource types. A term rule says which of the marketplace's own terms your resources are listed under when you send them there. Nothing changes until you approve it.

**Automations → Target terms** has five steps: **Where**, **Rule**, **What**, **Preview** and **Approve**.

## Step 1: Where

Choose where your resources come **From** and where they go **To**. The picture underneath shows the rules that are on for that pair, for example the word “unit” becoming Tes's **Unit of work**.

## Step 2: Rule

**Your term rules** lists the rules you have for this pair. Each one says what it sets, which resources it covers, and whether it is on. Use **Edit**, **Duplicate** or **Delete** on a rule.

**Suggestions** are ready-made rules. Press **Use this** to fill in the form with one, then check it before you save. A suggestion is only a starting point.

<!-- shot: /automations/mappings, step 2 Rule with two saved term rules and two suggestions -->
![Your term rules and suggestions](/v1/guides/images/c91b95d3c5903965989e30e49f00237cdaa13f4a9bfad1abce7a23bb14388445)

## Write a rule

1. Press **New rule**, or **Use this** on a suggestion.
2. Give it a **Name** and set it **On**.
3. Choose the **Licence on TES** or the **Resource type on TES** (or both).
4. Open **Only some resources?** to narrow it:
   - **Price on TPT**: any price, free resources only, or paid resources only.
   - **Words in the description**: words to look for, separated with commas. **The words must match** sets whether any one of them is enough or all of them are needed.
   - **Add a condition** matches on something else about the resource instead.
5. Press **Save this rule**.

<!-- shot: /automations/mappings, the rule form: resources whose description says "topic pack" or "unit" go under Unit of work on TES -->
![A term rule: descriptions with “topic pack” or “unit” go under Unit of work](/v1/guides/images/c716b4e17f7f1fd60971bbcf5e6719fb064fd6e028e62a5ca68b82c619ea1a69)

With no conditions, a rule covers every resource from that marketplace.

Only the words count when a rule looks at a title or description. Bold or other formatting is ignored.

## What you must set

- **Licence.** Each marketplace names its own licences. Pick the one you sell under. A resource with no licence for a marketplace is held back with the reason **that marketplace requires Licence**.
- **Resource type.** Match the types you use most. The rest can wait.

## Free licences

Some suggestions give free resources a Creative Commons licence, such as CC-BY-ND. The console marks these **Free licence: anyone may share it.** A Creative Commons licence lets anyone share copies of your resource, and you cannot take it back later. CC-BY-ND means people must credit you and must not change it. Only choose one for work you are happy to give away.

## Steps 3 and 4: What and Preview

1. In **What**, choose **Every resource on** the marketplace, or **Let me choose** and tick some.
2. In **Preview**, choose which rules to try: **Rules that are on**, **Only rules I tick**, or **Only the rule I am writing**.
3. Press **Preview**.

**One-off changes for this preview** lets you try a different rate, licence or resource type without changing any rule.

## Step 5: Approve

The table shows each resource with what it has on the target now and what is **Proposed**.

1. Tick the rows you want.
2. Press **Approve selected**, or **Approve all eligible** for every undecided row that is not blocked.
3. Press **Reject selected** or **Reject all undecided** for the ones you do not want.

A blocked row stays with its reason until you fix it or reject it.

## Unmatched words

When one of your resources uses a word Teachouse cannot match on a marketplace, it becomes a question under **Unmatched words**. Open it from the **Unmatched words** button on **Automations → Updates**.

1. Read the word and the marketplace it is for.
2. Type where it belongs, for example Mathematics / Algebra, and press **Save answer**.
3. Or press **Leave it out** if that marketplace has nothing like it.

You only answer each word once. Teachouse uses your answer every time it meets that word again.

## Templates fill, rules translate

A template fills in fields on a new resource. A term rule turns those fields into the marketplace's own terms. See [Templates](/guides/templates).
