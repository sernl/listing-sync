# Mapping your words to a marketplace's

Each marketplace has its own list of subjects, grades, licences and resource types. A target term rule says which of the marketplace's words your resources are listed under.

## Write a rule

1. Open **Automations → Target terms**.
2. Choose the marketplace.
3. Pick one of your words to match.
4. Choose the marketplace's word it becomes and save.

## What you must match

- **Licence.** Each marketplace names its own licences. Pick the one you sell under.
- **Resource type.** A marketplace will not accept a listing without one.
- **Subjects and grades.** Match the ones you use most. The rest can wait.

## Keyword matching

A rule can match a keyword in a title or description. Only the words count: bold or other formatting is ignored, so bold text matches the same as plain text.

## Unmatched words

If no rule covers a word, it shows up as an unmatched word.

1. Open **Unmatched words**.
2. Read the word and where it came from.
3. Choose the marketplace's word for it.

Once you answer a word, it stays answered. Teachouse uses your answer every time it meets that word again.

## Templates fill, rules translate

A template fills in fields on a new resource. A target term rule turns a filled-in field into the marketplace's own words. See [Templates](/guides/templates).
