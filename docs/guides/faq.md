# Pricing questions

## What is a move?

A move is publishing one imported resource onto one marketplace. Publishing a resource to Tes and TPT is 2 moves; to Tes alone is 1 move.

A move counts once, when you publish. Duplicates are merged first. A draft costs the same as a live listing.

## What is free?

Importing, editing in Teachouse, previewing and exporting never use a move. Only publishing a resource to a marketplace uses a move. On the free Look plan you also get 5 free moves when you connect your first shop.

## Do I have to subscribe?

No. Look is free for as long as you like. Buy a Move Pack on Look and use the moves whenever you like.

## Which plan do I want?

Pick by how often you publish: Starter gives 10 moves a month, Pro 25 and Studio 100. Moving your shop once? A Move Pack is cheaper than subscribing.

Starter and up show statistics for every shop. Pro and Studio also send your edits to every marketplace, schedule when listings go live, use term mapping and price rules, and can publish automatically. See [Your plan and what it allows](/guides/plans).

## What do the free moves give me?

You get 5 moves, once, when your first shop connects. Use them to publish onto any marketplace you choose. Connecting another shop does not add more.

## Do moves expire?

Pack moves last 12 months from the day you buy them. A plan's monthly moves build up to three months' worth: 30 on Starter, 75 on Pro and 300 on Studio. Once you reach that, a new month adds none.

**Account → Billing** shows the day your first moves expire, under **Your moves**.

## What happens if I run out of moves?

Nothing is charged by surprise. Your next move waits for next month's moves, or you buy a Move Pack.

## How many resources can I keep?

Look keeps up to 100 resources, Starter 250 and Pro 500. Studio has no limit.

If you go over, for example after moving to a smaller plan, you keep everything you have. You can't add more until you upgrade or make room.

## How many watermarked previews can I make?

Look makes 5 in all, for as long as you have your account. Starter makes 20 a month and Pro 50, and the count starts again on the 1st of each month. Studio has no limit.

## What happens to my moves if I cancel?

Your moves stay yours until they expire. Your plan stops adding moves when it ends.

## Can I change plans or cancel?

Yes, up or down at any time. Open **Account → Billing**:

- To change plan, press **Switch to …** on the plan you want. Stripe shows the new price before anything changes.
- To cancel, press **Cancel plan**. Your plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, and will not renew. **Keep my plan** undoes it until then.

Your resources and moves stay where they are.

## Can I edit a listing after I move it?

Yes. Within 90 days of a move, you can edit that listing once and send the change without using another move.

## Can I get a refund?

Yes, for a Move Pack you have not used: write to us within 14 days of buying it.

## Which marketplaces can I use?

Tes and Teachers Pay Teachers (TPT). You can move in either direction.

## Why do I need the Teachouse app?

TPT and Tes have no official way for other services to connect to them. So you connect them in the free Teachouse app, on your own device. The app signs in there for you, so Teachouse never holds your marketplace password.

You only need the app to connect those marketplaces. Importing, editing, publishing and everything else works in your browser too. The app does the marketplace work in the background, so keep it open on a device while an import or a move runs.

Get the app from [Marketplaces](/marketplaces), step 1 **Get the app**. There is an app for Windows, Linux and Android. There is no iPhone or Mac app yet. See [Why you need the Teachouse app](/guides/why-the-app).

## Do my files get uploaded to Teachouse?

Not the files you import. They stay on your own devices, and Teachouse keeps no copy. When you open, preview or download one in your browser, Teachouse passes it across from a device that has it and is online.

If every device that has the file is off, the console tells you which device it is on. Open the Teachouse app on that device and try again.

Files you upload in Teachouse yourself are different. Teachouse stores them so you can use them anywhere, until you delete them. **Catalogue → Files** shows where each file is. See [Your files](/guides/your-files).

## Can two people share an account?

Yes. You both sign in to the same Teachouse account, and each of you can install the Teachouse app on your own device. A marketplace shop can be connected to only one Teachouse account, so connect each shop to the account you share.

## I moved a listing and it failed. Did I lose a move?

No. A move counts only when the listing is published. If it never published, you are not charged.
