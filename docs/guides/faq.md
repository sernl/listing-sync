# Pricing questions

## What is a move?

A move is publishing one imported resource onto one marketplace. Publishing a resource to Tes and TPT is 2 moves; to Tes alone is 1 move.

A move counts once, when you publish. Duplicates are merged first. A draft costs the same as a live listing.

## What is free?

Importing, reading, editing in Teachouse, exporting and previewing are all free. Only publishing a resource to a marketplace uses a move.

## Do I have to subscribe?

No. Buy a Move Pack on the free Look plan and use the moves whenever you like.

## Move Packs or Sync — which do I want?

Buy a Move Pack if you are moving a shop once. Choose Sync if you list every week. Sync lets you edit resources in Teachouse and sync the edits to all your shops. It also shows statistics for every shop, runs schedules and gives you 25 moves a month.

## What do the free moves give me?

You get five moves for each shop, once, when the shop first connects. They stay yours for as long as you keep your account. A shop never gets a second set.

## Do moves expire?

Pack moves last 12 months from the day you buy them. Sync's monthly moves build up to 75. Once you have 75, a new month adds none.

## What happens to my moves if I cancel?

Moves you bought in a pack stay until they expire. Sync stops adding moves when your subscription ends.

## Can I edit a listing after I move it?

Yes. Within 90 days of a move, you can edit that listing once and send the change without using another move.

## Can I get a refund?

Write to us within 14 days of buying a pack. If you have not used any of its moves, we will refund it. You can cancel a subscription at any time in **Account → Plan and moves**. The current period is not refunded.

## What is Founding 100?

A yearly plan for the first 100 people who join. You pay $180 for the first year and $192 in years two and three. From year four you pay the list price. You also get 20 extra moves. It closes at 100 members or on 31 December 2026.

## What is "Move with me"?

A $99 session where we move your shop with you. It is a booking only, so buy a pack for the moves.

## Which marketplaces can I use?

Tes and Teachers Pay Teachers. You can move in either direction.

## Why do I need the desktop app?

Teachouse reaches Tes and TPT through your own signed-in browser on your own computer. The app does this for you. It keeps your marketplace password on your computer and reads your files where they already are.

## Do my files get uploaded to Teachouse?

No. Your files stay on your computers. They go to a marketplace only when you publish. The one exception is the thumbnail, which Teachouse keeps so it can show it to you.

## How many resources can I keep?

500 on the free Look plan. There is no limit on Sync.

## Can two people share an account?

Yes. Each marketplace shop still belongs to one account, so connect each shop once.

## I moved a listing and it failed. Did I lose a move?

No. A move counts only when the listing is published. If it never published, you are not charged.
