# Vendoo and the Teachouse console cross-reference

The founder's instruction is that our dashboard, naming convention and feature set should match Vendoo's, in our own colours.
This file answers that instruction: what Vendoo's structure actually is, where ours already matches, what is missing, what to rename, and the decisions only the founder can take.
Every Vendoo label below is quoted exactly from a public Vendoo page or its help centre; every claim about our console carries a file:line.

## How Vendoo is structured

Vendoo's marketing header carries "Features", "Marketplaces", "Pricing", "Enterprise", "Resources", "Contact Us" and "Help center", with the two calls to action "Start My Free Trial" and "Log in" (https://vendoo.co).
The "Features" dropdown is the product's own feature taxonomy in this order: "Crosslisting", "Sale detection and Auto Delist", "Bulk Actions", "Inventory Management", "Multi-Quantity", "Send Offers", "Analytics", "Mobile App", "Marketplace Sharing", "Size Chart Tool", "Marketplace Fee Calculator", "Profit Margin Calculator" (https://www.vendoo.co/).
The last three are free public calculators rather than gated product surface (https://www.vendoo.co/).

The in-app sidebar was regrouped in 2026 and now shows text labels for every section instead of icons alone, grouped by function (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/16091202-what-changed-in-vendoo-2026).
The 2026 article says "Marketplaces" now has its own place in main navigation, moved out of "Account Settings" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/16091202-what-changed-in-vendoo-2026), but the newer July 2026 Depop post gives the live path as "Settings -> Marketplace Connections" (https://blog.vendoo.co/new-depop-api-integration), and the connections article calls it the "Marketplace Connections Screen" inside the "Account Settings page" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260267-get-started-with-vendoo-create-an-account-download-the-extension-connect-your-marketplaces).
Read the newer source as live: connections are reached from settings, not from a top-level navigation entry, which is the claim founder question Q2 rested on.
A sidebar section named "Crosslist" holds "Labels", "Export CSV", "Template Manager" and "Duplicate Finder", each moved there by the article's own "Where did my tools go?" table (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/16091202-what-changed-in-vendoo-2026).
An earlier reading of the same article also placed "Import" and "Analytics" in that section (unverified: neither label was extractable from the cited article on re-read), so confirm both before the rename map's Crosslist group is built.
A sidebar section named "Automations", renamed from "Pro Tools", holds "Send Offers", "Auto Offers", "Marketplace Sharing" and "Stale Listing Warning" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/16091202-what-changed-in-vendoo-2026).
A help menu at top right holds "Get support", "Help Center" and "Tutorials" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/16091202-what-changed-in-vendoo-2026), and the "Status Page" was moved to the profile drop-down menu in April 2026 (https://blog.vendoo.co/vendoo-product-update-for-april-2026).

The central screen is called the "Inventory Page", also written "Inventory Screen" and "Inventory Management Screen", and it "lets you view your items, status, quantities, and essential information about your inventory" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260287-vendoo-inventory-management-tools).
Inventory is "divided into three columns based on their draughted, listed, or sold status", and "you can click each marketplace icon to take you directly to the live listing!" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260287-vendoo-inventory-management-tools).
The canonical marketplace-neutral record is "The Vendoo Form", carrying "Upload Photos", item details such as "title, description, brand, condition, colors, SKU, quantity, tags, etc.", "Category", "Shipping Information", "price", "cost of goods" and "additional private details" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260272-how-to-list-the-vendoo-form).
Beneath it sit per-item "marketplace forms" into which the Vendoo form's data "has transferred", leaving only marketplace-specific required fields "marked with red asterisks" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260260-how-to-list-items-with-vendoo).
The other named screens are the "Bulk Edit Screen" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/10438710-bulk-edit-edit-your-listings-in-bulk), the "Import" page (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260276-how-to-import-your-items-in-vendoo), the "Marketplace Connections Screen" inside the "Account Settings page" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260267-get-started-with-vendoo-create-an-account-download-the-extension-connect-your-marketplaces), the "Offers Dashboard" and "Auto Offers Manager" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/10043166-send-offers-to-likers-automatically-or-manually-in-bulk), the "Label Manager" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260284-vendoo-custom-labels-for-inventory-management), the "Template Manager" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260273-how-to-create-listing-templates-in-vendoo), the "sales detection dashboard" on the "sold tab" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/8047348-sale-detection-auto-delist) and the "subscription page" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260267-get-started-with-vendoo-create-an-account-download-the-extension-connect-your-marketplaces).

The primary verb is "Crosslist", used interchangeably with "Crosspost" in the extension's own store copy (https://www.vendoo.co/cross-listing-app and https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/vendoo-crosslist-extensio/mnampbajndaipakjhcbbaihllmghlcdf).
Vendoo's own coverage count is inconsistent, so do not copy it as a number: the store listing's short description says "crosslist to 10+ third party marketplaces" while its body says "Crosspost your items to 9 marketplaces", and the pricing comparison row says "Crossposting to 10+ marketplaces" (https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/vendoo-crosslist-extensio/mnampbajndaipakjhcbbaihllmghlcdf and https://www.vendoo.co/pricing).
The bulk menu sits "on the top right side of the inventory screen" and names "Bulk Delist & Relist", "Bulk Edit Vendoo Labels", "Bulk Copy Items", "Bulk Delete", "Bulk List", "Bulk Delist" and "Bulk Mark As Not Listed", with "Bulk Edit" documented separately (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/9777622-vendoo-bulk-actions), though Vendoo's own tutorial title is plural, "How to Use Vendoo's New Bulk Edits Feature" (unverified: the in-app label was not read from the Bulk Edit article) (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-QLEfH2LAko).
"Bulk Delete" is documented as "Permanently delete multiple items from your inventory at once", with the caution that "deleting items permanently removes them from Vendoo but does not delist them from marketplaces", and "Bulk Mark As Not Listed" "only removes the sync to the listing within Vendoo" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/9777622-vendoo-bulk-actions).
The Bulk Edit Screen's two exit verbs are "Save and relist" and "Save Only", and Vendoo states that "your changes will not be reflected on your live listings unless you save and relist" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/10438710-bulk-edit-edit-your-listings-in-bulk).
The per-field reconciliation controls are "Update All" on the Vendoo form and "Reset" on a marketplace form, each appearing only where that field has diverged (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/9128864-how-do-the-update-all-and-reset-buttons-work).
The per-marketplace status dropdown carries "Mark as Listed", which requires pasting "the URL to the active listing", and "Mark As Sold", which opens a sales-details form (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260269-how-to-handle-items-that-have-been-imported-into-vendoo-but-are-already-listed-on-multiple-marketplaces and https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260270-how-to-mark-items-as-sold-on-vendoo).
Other named verbs are "Get Latest Items" on the import screen (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260276-how-to-import-your-items-in-vendoo), "Add Sales Details" after a detected sale (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/8047348-sale-detection-auto-delist), "Create with AI" under the description field (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/12578441-ai-listing-enhancement), "Copy" then "Confirm" on the row's three-dot menu (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260274-how-do-i-create-copies-of-items), and the lowercase "connect" under each marketplace (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260267-get-started-with-vendoo-create-an-account-download-the-extension-connect-your-marketplaces).
Relist is delete-and-recreate rather than an edit: Vendoo states that "it entirely deletes your listing from a marketplace and creates a brand-new listing", "a brand-new, fresh listing" "with a new URL", "not a copy or a duplicate" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260292-how-to-use-vendoo-s-delist-relist-feature).

The item-level status vocabulary on the inventory page is three words, "draughted, listed, or sold" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260287-vendoo-inventory-management-tools).
A fourth item state exists outside that page: the April 2026 fixes list items being "incorrectly marked as Delisted" (https://blog.vendoo.co/vendoo-product-update-for-april-2026).
Per-marketplace status is separate and reads "Not Listed" or "listed" on each marketplace row inside an item (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260269-how-to-handle-items-that-have-been-imported-into-vendoo-but-are-already-listed-on-multiple-marketplaces).
Failure and attention states are rendered as symbols rather than words: a "yellow warning symbol" for an item "pending sales details", a "red warning symbol" when a sold item failed to delist elsewhere, and a "blue notification dot" on the Sold tab when a sale is detected (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/8047348-sale-detection-auto-delist).
Stale items get "a yellow tab with a clock icon" whose hover "displays the number of days the item has been listed on the marketplace" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260291-vendoo-s-stale-inventory-system).
The import screen carries a "Not Imported" tab, selected by default (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6759976-when-i-import-my-items-are-not-all-showing-up-on-the-import-screen).
The settings page confirms the client dependency with the status text "Vendoo Extension Installed" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260267-get-started-with-vendoo-create-an-account-download-the-extension-connect-your-marketplaces).
"BETA" is a user-visible label Vendoo ships rather than hides, currently on "Import & Merge", "Bulk Edit", sale detection, Send Offers, Marketplace Sharing and Whatnot (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260300-which-marketplaces-does-vendoo-support and https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/10438710-bulk-edit-edit-your-listings-in-bulk).
Two non-success words do appear in the newer sources: the item state "Delisted", and the connection status "Not Connected" beside a "Connect Depop" button reached by "Settings -> Marketplace Connections" (https://blog.vendoo.co/vendoo-product-update-for-april-2026 and https://blog.vendoo.co/new-depop-api-integration).
Beyond those, no error, failed, queued or needs-reauthentication state is named in any Vendoo page found, so their per-marketplace write failures surface as symbols and troubleshooting articles rather than as a status vocabulary (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6371675-delist-relist-troubleshooting).

The published tiers are "Starter" at $14.99 monthly or $12.49 billed yearly, "Growth" at $29.99 or $24.99 and marked "Recommended", "Pro" at $59.99 or $49.99, and "Enterprise" at "Contact Sales" for businesses "creating over 1,000 new monthly listings" (https://www.vendoo.co/pricing).
A "Pay Monthly" and "Pay Yearly" toggle, marked "(Up to 2 months free)", switches between two cards that do not carry the same bullets, so a feature list copied from one is wrong for the other (https://www.vendoo.co/pricing).
Starter carries "Unlimited Items", "All Marketplaces", "Sale Detection and Auto Delisting", "Listing Templates", "Importing", "Delist & Relist", "Analytics" and "Mobile app iOS and Android", with "Live Customer Support 7 Days a Week" on the yearly card; Growth adds "AI Listing Enhancement", "Bulk Actions: Manage up to 240 listings at once" and "Up to 300 PhotoRoom Background Removals"; monthly Pro adds "Auto Send Offers on 6+ marketplaces", "Marketplace Sharing: Poshmark, Depop & Grailed.", "Up to 1500 PhotoRoom Background Removals" and "Listing Videos", while yearly Pro reads "Automatic & Manual send offers on 6 marketplaces", "Listing Videos for Poshmark and eBay" and "Premium Support & Live Call Support" (https://www.vendoo.co/pricing).
The gating exception is that "Bulk actions are included in all plans" while "Bulk Delist & Relist, Bulk List and Bulk Delist are add-ons", and "Annual plans have all add-ons included!" (https://www.vendoo.co/bulk-actions-feature).
The only multi-seat construct anywhere is Enterprise "Admin Roles" with "3 Users Included" and "($27 Additional Seats)"; no self-serve tier advertises roles, seats or teams (https://www.vendoo.co/enterprise/high-volume).
Two cautions before copying the ladder: the same pricing page still publishes a legacy item-metered scheme from "FREE plan" $0 for 5 items up to "Expert plan" $149.99 for 4000 items, without saying which is live (https://www.vendoo.co/pricing), and connecting Shopify carries a separate "$9.99 monthly charge" because "Shopify requires all apps in their App Store to use Shopify Billing" (https://www.vendoo.co/marketplaces).

## What the newest sources add

Vendoo now runs one marketplace server-side through an official API and the rest through its extension, which is the two-branch split this product already committed to.
Its Depop integration runs on the official Depop API, where "listing, delisting, importing, syncing, Sale Detection, sharing, and sending offers all run directly through Vendoo" with "no browser extension required", while the same post tells sellers "If you sell on other marketplaces that use the extension, keep it installed for those" (https://blog.vendoo.co/new-depop-api-integration).
The migration is a stated roadmap rather than a one-off: April 2026 names an upcoming "Mercari & Depop API Connection" and an upcoming "Etsy Sale Detection" (https://blog.vendoo.co/vendoo-product-update-for-april-2026).
On the API branch Vendoo "syncs your Depop items automatically every 30 minutes, in the background", surfaced by a "Last sync" label on the Import page (https://blog.vendoo.co/new-depop-api-integration).
What Vendoo does not do is tell the seller which branch a marketplace is on: the marketplaces page lists Shopify, Whatnot, Grailed, eBay, Vinted, Depop, Poshmark, FBMP, Mercari, VC and Etsy with no "API", "extension required" or "BETA" annotation, and the word API appears only in trademark disclaimers such as "This application uses the eBay API but is not endorsed or certified by eBay, Inc." (https://www.vendoo.co/marketplaces).

The extension is listed as "Vendoo Crosslist Extension v3", version "3.1.10", "Updated September 26, 2025", with "70,000 users" and a 3.5 rating from "69 ratings" (https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/vendoo-crosslist-extensio/mnampbajndaipakjhcbbaihllmghlcdf).
It has not shipped in roughly a year while the API branch advanced, which corroborates the migration, and its one-line custody statement is "This extension allows Vendoo to access and communicate with your marketplaces", alongside three data declarations including "Not being sold to third parties, outside of the approved use cases" (https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/vendoo-crosslist-extensio/mnampbajndaipakjhcbbaihllmghlcdf).

The pricing page is built on a free trial rather than on the tiers: every tier's call to action is "Start Your Free Trial", the marketing header's is "Start My Free Trial", and four of the eight FAQ titles govern it, "How does the Vendoo free trial work?", "Do I need a credit card to start?", "What happens when my trial ends?" and "What is Vendoo's refund policy?", the last carrying the subheadings "Standard Refund Eligibility" and "Free Trial Policy" (https://www.vendoo.co/pricing and https://vendoo.co).
Four rows of "Compare Plans and Features" have no counterpart in this document's earlier reading: "Inventory" valued "Unlimited", "Crossposting to 10+ marketplaces", "Live Customer Support 7 Days a Week" with the Pro-tier "Premium Support & Live Call Support", and the value "Individual user analytics" (https://www.vendoo.co/pricing).
Vendoo also meters a consumable and sells more of it inside the product: a "Background Remover Counter" shows "how many background removals you have used of your monthly allowance", and "You can purchase 20 additional listings at any time in your Account Settings for only $3.19, which will come with 20 more background removals" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/8011114-photo-editing-tools-background-remover-by-photoroom).
The referral terms confirm top-ups are a live revenue line by paying commission on "monthly add-on purchases" (https://www.vendoo.co/referral-program).

Two product surfaces this document had not recorded sit inside the listing flow.
The first is a photo editor reached from the Vendoo Form, whose instruction is to "select Remove Background, making sure you're in the Vendoo Form, not a Marketplace form", carrying an "Undo All Image Edits" control that does not refund the counter, and its own "Vendoo Photo Editor Tutorial" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/8011114-photo-editing-tools-background-remover-by-photoroom and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OiQqkGbzYVs).
The second is "Default Preferences", paired with templates in the comparison row "Custom Listing Templates & Default Preferences" and tutorialised separately as "Customize your Vendoo Settings For a Platform That's All Your Own!" (https://www.vendoo.co/pricing and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OuWZQ_8jWOo).

The listing form itself is versioned and marketplace-anchored: February 2026 added a "Describe Your Style" field and updated the condition field "so options now match Poshmark exactly, ensuring smoother syncing" (https://blog.vendoo.co/vendoo-product-updates-for-february-2026).
The Depop page names "Price Suggestions", which "show you what similar items actually sold for as you list" (https://www.vendoo.co/marketplaces/resell-on-depop).
April 2026 added "Smart Sell" for Poshmark, auto-accepting, countering or ignoring offers, and improved duplicate detection on the import screen, whose own name is "Import & Merge" (https://blog.vendoo.co/vendoo-product-update-for-april-2026 and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7tr9FmcqdU0).
"Account Settings" remains a named screen, hosting the add-on purchase and formerly hosting "Marketplaces", "Labels", "Export CSV" and "Stale Listing Warning" (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/16091202-what-changed-in-vendoo-2026 and https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/8011114-photo-editing-tools-background-remover-by-photoroom).

Onboarding, changelog and growth are product surfaces too.
The 2026 changes article opens with a "Take a Quick Tour" control, and Vendoo ships a "Vendoo Onboarding Tutorial" alongside a biweekly live tutorial call (https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/16091202-what-changed-in-vendoo-2026 and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOChOK3ZTSc).
Monthly "Product Updates" posts under a "Vendoo Updates" tag are how its users learn what changed, and a "Connect with the Vendoo Community" help collection carries the community surface (https://blog.vendoo.co/tag/vendoo-updates and https://help.vendoo.co/en/collections/3488388-connect-with-the-vendoo-community).
Growth runs on "Refer-A-Friend" with a "Join the Program" control and "Silver or Gold Tier" status for "the top 10% of affiliates", a separate ShareASale "Affiliate Program", "Become a Vendoo Ambassador" and an Enterprise referral bounty (https://www.vendoo.co/referral-program and https://www.vendoo.co/affiliate-program-1 and https://www.vendoo.co/become-a-vendoo-ambassador and https://www.vendoo.co/enterprise/referral), with public coupon codes alongside them (unverified: no source URL to hand).

## Mobile app

Vendoo ships one native app on both platforms, "Vendoo: A Seller's Best Friend" by "Vendoo Inc.", at version 3.2.7, package co.vendoo.mobile [ios, play].
Its App Store feature list is "Create and edit listings in seconds", "Upload photos directly from your phone", "Track revenue, profit, and sales analytics", "Organize and manage your inventory", "Crosspost across multiple marketplaces" and "Delist, relist, and mark items as sold or not listed" [ios].
The same developer also ships a second, separate app, "Vendoo Go", tagged "Snap. List. Sell. Vendoo it!", so the phone surface is not necessarily one binary [ios].
The Play listing renders its description in script and could not be read as text, so nothing below rests on it alone [play].

No source found names Vendoo's bottom navigation or tab bar, because the help centre teaches mobile navigation through screenshots rather than prose.
What the text does establish is that a "Settings" destination exists on the phone and contains marketplace connections, since the setup article's one mobile line reads "To connect your marketplaces on the mobile app, go to Settings > Marketplaces" [h-start].
Beyond that only in-item navigation is named: a "marketplaces button", written elsewhere as a "pink Marketplaces button", opens a marketplace selection screen from inside an item, and a "pink down arrow in the bottom right corner" opens the per-marketplace menu carrying "Mark as Sold", "delist item" and "relist item" [h-sold, h-mobrelist].
That corner is the only positional fact about the app's chrome any source states in words.

The screens reachable on the phone are therefore attested by capability rather than by name.
Inventory is one, since "Inventory management is also available on the Vendoo mobile app!", and its web counterpart is called the "Inventory Page" and elsewhere the "inventory screen" [h-inv].
An item detail screen exists, reached from inventory and carrying the marketplaces button above [h-sold].
Analytics, a photo editor with background removal, a draft-creation path, "Customer Service Chat" and "Help Center" are each attested as present, but no source names the screen that hosts any of them [h-phone].

What an inventory card shows on the phone is stated nowhere found.
The nearest evidence describes the web Inventory Page, where a seller can "view your items, status, quantities, and essential information" and "click each marketplace icon to take you directly to the live listing", which establishes per-item marketplace icons and a per-item status on the web and not on the phone [h-inv].
That article divides inventory into "three columns" by drafted, listed or sold status, and the App Store copy adds a fourth term in "mark items as sold or not listed" [h-inv, ios].
Whether the phone renders those statuses as tabs, as a filter, or as the same columns is unknown.

Every verb in the brief is settled by one help-centre article, "Can I use Vendoo from my phone or tablet?", written 9 July 2026, which carries two explicit lists [h-phone].
Available on the app: "Creating Drafts", "Photo Editing & Background Removal", "Listing & Crosslisting", "Inventory Management", "Custom Labels", "Delist/Relist (Individual)", "Mark As Sold & Multi-Delist", "Accessing Analytics", "Customer Service Chat" and "Help Center" [h-phone].
Not available on the app: "Importing, Bulk Delist/Relist, Sale Detection and Auto Delist, CSV Download, Stale Listing Warning System, Facebook Marketplace, and Shopify" [h-phone].
Two corrections to the brief follow.
Multi-delist is on the phone, bundled with mark-as-sold rather than with bulk actions, so the line runs between one item across its marketplaces and many items at once, not between single and multiple [h-phone].
The exclusion covers two marketplaces outright and not only verbs, since Facebook Marketplace and Shopify have no mobile surface at all, which is why the mark-as-sold article warns that some delisting must finish on the desktop site [h-phone, h-sold].
The same boundary is stated positively for setup: "you must use the computer to get started, because you cannot import from the mobile app or mobile website" [h-phone].

Two Vendoo pages contradict the help centre, and one contradicts itself.
The mobile marketing page's FAQ puts sale detection among the features available in the app while also saying "your computer must be on and connected to the marketplaces for sale detection and auto delist to work", and the help article lists sale detection as unavailable on the app [mobile, h-phone].
The general FAQ page grants the phone "send offers", which the help article's available list omits [faq-all].
Prefer the help article on both counts: it is the most specific source, it is dated 9 July 2026, and it alone is written as an availability matrix.

Independent written sources add little structure.
A two-year user review says the app "mirrors this desktop experience" and is "surprisingly robust for quick edits or checking stats on the fly", yet ties every dashboard detail to the desktop login and names no mobile tab, screen or card [closo].
An App Store reviewer frames the app as an accessory to the web version rather than a replacement [ios].
A third review, SellerAider's, covers the desktop product only and says nothing about the app [selleraider].

For the implementer the sourced structural facts are these, and only these: a Settings destination holding Marketplaces; an inventory screen; an item detail screen whose marketplace actions hang off a pink control at the bottom right; a status vocabulary of drafted, listed and sold, with "not listed" as a fourth term in store copy; per-item marketplace icons opening the live listing, attested on the web only; and the verb split above, which is exact and datable.
Unknown, and not to be invented: the bottom tab bar and its labels, how many tabs there are, the name of the app's entry screen, whether a home or dashboard screen exists on the phone at all, the fields and layout of a phone inventory card, whether status appears as tabs or as a filter, and where analytics, drafts and the photo editor sit in the navigation.
Those gaps all exist for one reason, that the sources carry them in screenshots rather than text, so the cheap way to close them is a founder screen-recording of the app rather than further searching.

## What stays ours

Colours, typography, spacing and every component stay the console's own, and no Vendoo text, image, stylesheet or layout is copied into this product.
Only three things align: the structure of the navigation, the names of screens and states, and which features exist.

## Founder questions

Q1. Adopt "Inventory" as the nav word and "item" as the single seller-facing noun, retiring the current split between "resource" on the board and "listing" in the create flow.
Recommended: yes, and take the route change to /inventory in the same commit, because the redirect mechanism already exists and a second rename later costs the same work twice.

Q2. Merge /connections and /settings/devices into one Marketplaces screen showing both transport branches on one row per marketplace.
Recommended: yes, because Vendoo has one connections screen and our seller has one question, which is whether this marketplace can be written to right now.
Note that Vendoo's own newest source puts that screen under "Settings -> Marketplace Connections" rather than in main navigation, so the merge is the decision and the placement is open.

Q3. Which Vendoo bulk verbs do we adopt, given delist-and-relist is refused.
Recommended: bulk edit, bulk cross-list, bulk labels, bulk delete and bulk mark-as-listed, and never a bulk delist-and-relist under any name.

Q4. Adopt Vendoo's tier names Starter, Growth, Pro and Enterprise while keeping D4's metering axis of connected marketplaces.
Recommended: yes to the names, no to the axis, and do not copy the add-on ladder, which is the part of Vendoo's pricing its own page contradicts.

Q5. Build order for the four missing features that block a Vendoo-shaped console: import, cross-list-to-a-new-marketplace (G1), the live listing URL (G3), and custom labels (G6).
Recommended: import first, then G1, then G3, then labels, because a seller with no catalogue in the tool cannot use any of the other three.

Q6. Whether one organisation may hold more than one person, which Vendoo offers only on Enterprise as "Admin Roles".
Recommended: no for launch, and revisit only when a school or a co-selling pair asks, because seats touch authorisation in Postgres rather than the console.

Q7. The instruction to match Vendoo collides with three standing decisions: no sold column, no delist-and-relist, no auto-delist, recorded at docs/notes/design/seller-dashboard.md:17-20.
Recommended: the decisions stand and the instruction is read as covering structure, names and the feature set rather than the physical-goods mechanics, which is how this document has read it throughout.
