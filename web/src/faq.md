# Pricing questions

## What is a move?

A move is one resource committed to the other marketplace. It is counted once, when you commit it, after duplicates are merged, and a draft costs the same as a live listing.

## What does not cost a move?

Importing, reading, editing in Teachouse, exporting and previewing all cost nothing. Only committing a resource to a marketplace does.

## Do I have to subscribe?

No. Buy a pack of moves on the free plan and use them whenever you like.

## Packs or Sync — which do I want?

Buy a pack if you are moving a shop once. Take Sync if you list every week and want schedules, pulls and figures as well as 25 moves a month.

## What do the free moves get me?

Five moves for each shop, given once when the shop first connects. They are yours for as long as the account lives, and a shop never gets a second set.

## Do moves expire?

Pack moves are good for 12 months from the day you buy them. Sync's monthly moves stack up to 75; above that, a new month adds none.

## What happens to my moves if I cancel?

Moves you bought in a pack stay until they expire. Moves that came with Sync stop accruing when the subscription ends.

## Can I edit a listing after I move it?

Yes. For 90 days after a move you can edit that listing and send the change without spending another move.

## Can I get a refund?

Write to us within 14 days of buying a pack and we will refund it if the moves are unspent. Cancel a subscription at any time from **Account → Subscription**; the current period is not refunded.

## What is Founding 100?

An annual plan for the first 100 people who take it: $180 for the first year, $192 in years two and three, list price from year four, and 20 moves on top. It closes at 100 members or on 31 December 2026.

## What is "Move with me"?

A $99 session where we do your move with you. It is a booking, so buy a pack for the moves themselves.

## Which marketplaces can I use?

Tes and Teachers Pay Teachers, in both directions.

## Why do I need the desktop app?

Tes and TPT are reached through your own signed-in browser session on your own machine. The app provides that session, keeps your marketplace password on your machine, and reads your files where they already are.

## Do my files get uploaded to Teachouse?

No. Files stay on your machines and are sent to a marketplace only when you publish. The one exception is the thumbnail, which is stored so the console can show it.

## How many resources can I keep?

500 on the free plan, and no limit on Sync.

## Can two people share an account?

Yes, and a marketplace shop still belongs to one account. Connect each shop once.

## I moved a listing and it failed — was a move spent?

No. A move is counted when the listing is committed. A row that never committed is not charged.
